# Project Vision

The Lawn With Care Universe is an educational entertainment franchise centered on curiosity, scientific thinking, observation and respect for nature.

The stories take place in the real world.

Nothing magical is added.

The extraordinary comes from paying attention.

Nature is always the protagonist.

# Universe Philosophy

The franchise is built upon a few central ideas.

Nature is the protagonist.

Wonder comes before knowledge.

Curiosity creates care.

Scientific thinking begins with observation.

Children deserve honest answers.

Adults continue learning.

Questions are more valuable than immediate answers.

Every backyard is an ecosystem.

Every ecosystem tells stories.
