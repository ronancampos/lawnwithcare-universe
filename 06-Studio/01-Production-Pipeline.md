# 🌱 Lawn With Care Universe

# Production Pipeline

> "A great story is not created in one step.
> It is refined through a disciplined process."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official production workflow for every asset created within the Lawn With Care Universe.

Its objective is to ensure consistency, quality, scalability and continuity across all formats.

The pipeline applies to:

• Comics

• Picture books

• Instagram carousels

• Facebook posts

• Animated shorts

• Educational materials

• Marketing content

• Merchandise concepts

• Internal documentation

---

# Production Philosophy

The franchise is documentation-driven.

Ideas do not become canon immediately.

Every creative asset passes through a structured production pipeline.

The process is designed to protect consistency while encouraging creativity.

---

# Core Principles

Every production should be:

Canon-first

Story-driven

Scientifically credible

Educational without feeling instructional

Visually consistent

Emotionally warm

Reusable

Version-controlled

---

# Production Workflow

Every project follows the same sequence.

```
Idea
    ↓
Concept Brief
    ↓
Scientific Validation
    ↓
Episode Brief
    ↓
Story Outline
    ↓
Storyboard
    ↓
Dialogue
    ↓
Art Direction
    ↓
Illustration
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Educational Review
    ↓
Canon Review
    ↓
Publication
    ↓
Archive
```

No stage should be skipped without justification.

---

# Stage 01 — Idea

Goal

Capture an observation worth exploring.

Sources may include:

Nature

Real experiences

Scientific papers

Daily life

Children's questions

Seasonal events

Customer interactions

Personal observations

Deliverable

One paragraph describing the idea.

---

# Stage 02 — Concept Brief

Purpose

Determine whether the idea belongs in the franchise.

Questions

Is curiosity the starting point?

Does nature drive the story?

Can children observe this in real life?

Does it reinforce franchise values?

Deliverable

Concept Brief.

---

# Stage 03 — Scientific Validation

Purpose

Verify scientific accuracy.

Scientific simplification is allowed.

Scientific distortion is not.

Deliverable

Reference notes.

Scientific sources.

Terminology.

---

# Stage 04 — Episode Brief

Purpose

Summarize the episode.

Includes

Title

Core Question

Learning Goal

Setting

Season

Discovery Badge

Main Observation

Estimated length

Target audience

Deliverable

Episode Brief.

---

# Stage 05 — Story Outline

Purpose

Create the narrative skeleton.

Based on the official Episode Structure.

Must include

Curiosity Hook

Observation

Question

Investigation

Understanding

Care

Reflection

Reader Challenge

Deliverable

Scene outline.

---

# Stage 06 — Storyboard

Purpose

Visual planning.

Defines

Composition

Camera

Discovery Moments™

Page turns

Instagram slides

Dialogue placement

Visual rhythm

Deliverable

Storyboard sketches.

---

# Stage 07 — Dialogue

Purpose

Write natural conversations.

Dialogue follows:

Dialogue.md

Story Bible

Character Canon

Characters never speak outside their established personalities.

Deliverable

Dialogue script.

---

# Stage 08 — Art Direction

Purpose

Prepare visual references.

Includes

Character references

Locations

Equipment

Color palette

Season

Lighting

Mood

Deliverable

Art Package.

---

# Stage 09 — Illustration

Purpose

Create final artwork.

Illustrators follow:

Character Guides

Visual DNA

Design Rules

World Bible

Field Equipment

Illustrations never redefine canon.

Deliverable

Master artwork.

---

# Stage 10 — Internal Review

Purpose

Quality control.

Checklist

Visual consistency

Dialogue

Composition

Educational clarity

Continuity

Deliverable

Review Report.

---

# Stage 11 — Educational Review

Purpose

Confirm educational integrity.

Questions

Does curiosity come before information?

Is science accurate?

Is the tone encouraging?

Could a child repeat the observation?

Deliverable

Educational approval.

---

# Stage 12 — Canon Review

Purpose

Protect franchise continuity.

Questions

Does the story contradict existing canon?

Are characters behaving consistently?

Are recurring props correct?

Are locations accurate?

Deliverable

Canon approval.

---

# Stage 13 — Publication

Possible outputs

Instagram Carousel

Facebook

Comic Book

Picture Book

Website

PDF

Educational Kit

Animation

All formats derive from the same approved master.

---

# Stage 14 — Archive

Purpose

Preserve every production artifact.

Archive includes

Episode Brief

Storyboard

Dialogue

Illustrations

Exported assets

Prompt history

Review notes

Version history

Nothing is discarded.

# Change Management

Changes should be categorized as:

Editorial

Visual

Scientific

Narrative

Canon

Major canon changes require documentation updates.

---

# Quality Gates

Every production must answer "Yes" to the following:

✓ Does it respect the Story Bible?

✓ Does it respect Character Canon?

✓ Does it respect the World Bible?

✓ Does it respect scientific integrity?

✓ Does it inspire curiosity?

✓ Would Brooks behave this way?

✓ Would Sprout say this?

✓ Does it make the audience want to explore nature?

If any answer is "No",

the production returns for revision.
