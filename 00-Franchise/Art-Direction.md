# 🌱 Lawn With Care Universe

# Art Direction

---

> **Our illustrations do not exaggerate reality. They reveal its beauty.**

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official artistic direction of the Lawn With Care Universe.

It establishes the visual standards that every illustration, comic, animation, concept art and AI-generated image must follow.

This is not a style guide for one artist.

It is the visual language of the franchise.

---

# Artistic Vision

The Lawn With Care Universe should feel as if every illustration were created with affection for nature.

The artwork must invite observation.

Readers should instinctively slow down.

Look closer.

Smile.

Notice details.

Nothing should feel rushed.

Nothing should feel visually aggressive.

---

# Visual Identity

The franchise combines:

• cinematic composition

• children's book warmth

• modern animation appeal

• scientific clarity

• environmental storytelling

Every illustration should feel simultaneously educational and magical without becoming fantasy.

---

# Artistic Influences

The emotional references are:

Pixar

DreamWorks

Studio Ghibli (sense of wonder only)

Classic illustrated children's books

National Park interpretation panels

Nature journals

Scientific field sketchbooks

---

The visual result must remain original.

Never imitate another franchise.

Never reproduce copyrighted visual language.

---

# Rendering Style

Rendering is stylized.

Not realistic.

Not painterly.

Not comic-book ink heavy.

Characteristics:

Soft transitions

Minimal gradients

Flat local colors

Warm ambient lighting

Gentle atmospheric perspective

Readable silhouettes

Simple textures

---

# Illustration Goals

Every illustration should communicate at least one of these:

Wonder

Curiosity

Discovery

Care

Calm

Friendship

Respect

Hope

---

# Composition

Every composition must answer:

Where should the eye look first?

Where should it travel second?

What should the audience discover last?

The illustration should reward careful observation.

---

# Camera Language

The camera behaves like a curious explorer.

Preferred angles:

Eye level

Low angle through grass

Close to insects

Beside flowers

Under tree branches

Looking upward into canopies

Ground-level perspective

Avoid excessive aerial shots.

Avoid dramatic superhero angles.

---

# Scale

Small discoveries deserve large compositions.

A tiny beetle may occupy an entire page.

Importance is emotional.

Not physical.

---

# Characters

Characters belong to nature.

They never dominate the landscape.

Nature is always slightly larger than the individual.

This reinforces humility and respect.

---

# Backgrounds

Backgrounds are living ecosystems.

Every environment should suggest life.

Wind.

Birds.

Seeds.

Leaves.

Cloud movement.

Light.

Nothing static.

Nothing empty.

---

# Nature

Nature is never "generic."

Every location should tell a story.

Different grasses.

Different flowers.

Different tree species.

Different soils.

Different weather.

Ecology matters.

---

# Color Harmony

Primary colors come from the official palette.

Additional colors must feel naturally occurring.

Avoid:

Neon colors

Artificial saturation

Extreme contrast

Cold corporate palettes

---

# Lighting

Light communicates emotion.

Morning

Discovery

Freshness

Curiosity

---

Afternoon

Activity

Learning

Movement

---

Golden Hour

Reflection

Gratitude

Completion

---

Rain

Observation

Patience

Quietness

---

Snow

Silence

Rest

Wonder

---

# Shadows

Soft.

Readable.

Never harsh.

Never frightening.

Children should always feel emotionally safe.

---

# Line Quality

Primary outlines:

Medium thickness.

Internal details:

Thin.

Decorative lines:

Minimal.

Never over-render.

---

# Expressions

Expressions are subtle.

Warm.

Believable.

Never exaggerated to the point of caricature.

Children should recognize genuine emotions.

---

# Movement

Everything moves naturally.

Plants bend with wind.

Children bounce lightly.

Brooks walks with confidence.

Birds move like birds.

Nothing feels mechanical.

---

# Storytelling Through Art

Every illustration should answer:

What happened just before this moment?

What will happen immediately after?

Good illustrations contain invisible stories.

---

# Environmental Storytelling

Objects should reveal previous activity.

Footprints.

Bent grass.

A fallen feather.

A recently opened pine cone.

A muddy boot print.

These details enrich the world.

---

# Discovery Frame™

The Discovery Frame is a storytelling device.

It should only appear when focused observation is important.

Never use it as decoration.

Never overuse it.

Its appearance should immediately slow the rhythm of the page.

---

# Visual Rhythm

Alternate between:

Wide landscapes.

Medium interactions.

Extreme close-ups.

Quiet moments.

Action moments.

The page should breathe.

---

# Scientific Accuracy

Simplification is acceptable.

Scientific distortion is not.

Species should remain recognizable.

Habitats should remain believable.

Seasonal behavior should remain coherent.

---

# Things We Never Do

No horror aesthetics.

No grotesque insects.

No exaggerated danger.

No unnecessary destruction.

No cynical humor.

No military visual language.

No visual violence.

No corporate advertisements inside stories.

---

# AI Generation Standards

Every AI-generated image must preserve:

Character proportions.

Official palette.

Discovery Frame.

Artistic warmth.

Rounded organic shapes.

Nature-first composition.

Soft cinematic lighting.

Educational tone.

---

# Final Test

Hide the title.

Hide the logo.

Hide the dialogue.

If someone still says:

"This looks like Lawn With Care."

The artwork succeeded.

---

# Official Art Direction Statement

Art exists to help people notice beauty that was always there.
