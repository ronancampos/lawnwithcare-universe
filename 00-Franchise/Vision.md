# 🌱 Lawn With Care Universe

# Vision

---

> **"Every backyard has a story. Every story begins with care."**

---

# Purpose

The Lawn With Care Universe exists to inspire children and families to rediscover the extraordinary hidden inside ordinary nature.

Our stories transform backyards, parks and neighborhoods into places of curiosity, science, adventure and imagination.

We believe that children naturally care about what they truly understand.

Understanding begins with observation.

Observation begins with curiosity.

Curiosity changes the world.

---

# Why This Franchise Exists

Modern children spend less time observing the natural world.

Many know the names of dinosaurs and planets but have never looked closely at the moss growing beside a sidewalk.

Many can identify fictional monsters but cannot recognize the insects living in their own backyard.

The Lawn With Care Universe seeks to reconnect children with the living world around them.

Not through lectures.

Not through fear.

Not through obligation.

But through wonder.

---

# Our Promise

Every story will leave the audience seeing the world differently than before.

Children should finish an episode wanting to go outside.

Parents should finish an episode wanting to explore with their children.

Teachers should finish an episode with new opportunities for conversation.

Then they learn.

Optimistic.

# Scientific Philosophy

Not as a collection of answers.

Ranger Brooks does not know everything.

Mistakes are welcomed.

Observation always comes first.
