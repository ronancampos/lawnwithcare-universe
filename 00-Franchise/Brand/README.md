## Official Asset Authority

The logo files listed above are the official Lawn With Care brand assets.

They are the source of truth for all brand applications within the Lawn With Care Universe.

Official artwork must not be:

- redrawn;
- reconstructed;
- distorted;
- mirrored;
- simplified;
- reinterpreted;
- regenerated by AI.

Cropping excess transparent or empty border space is permitted when required for production, provided the internal artwork remains unchanged.

## AI Production

AI-generated reproductions of Lawn With Care logos are not considered official artwork.

When exact branding is required:

1. create the illustration with the appropriate brand placement;
2. use one of the official source assets from this directory;
3. composite the official logo/icon into the artwork;
4. verify proportions and placement before final export.

The official files stored here always take precedence over AI-generated approximations.
