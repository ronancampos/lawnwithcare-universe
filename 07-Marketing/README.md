# 07 --- Marketing

This folder holds outward-facing publication guidance for the Lawn
With Care Universe: social account setup, caption voice, and
publishing workflows. It governs *how approved episode content gets
published*, not the content itself.

It does not define franchise, character, or story canon --- those
remain governed by `00-Franchise/`, `01-Characters/`, and
`03-Storytelling/`. Marketing copy must stay consistent with
`Storytelling-DNA.md`'s tone but is not itself canon; treat it as
`APPROVED` operational guidance that can evolve with less ceremony
than character or world canon.
