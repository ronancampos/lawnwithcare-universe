# Instagram Guidelines

## Purpose

Everything needed to set up and publish the Lawn With Care Universe's
Instagram account consistently, without re-deriving voice or format
decisions for every post. Covers: account setup, caption voice and
formula, hashtag strategy, and the publishing checklist.

This is `APPROVED` operational guidance, not franchise canon --- it can
be revised as the account grows without a canon-change process. It
must stay consistent with `Storytelling-DNA.md`'s tone (see
"Caption Voice" below), which *is* canon.

---

**Handle:** `@yardranger.lwc`

## Caption Voice

Caption copy is **narrator/brand voice**, not Brooks' or Sprout's
dialogue voice (see `01-Characters/Brooks/12-Voice.md` and
`01-Characters/Sprout/12-Voice.md` for their character voices, which
govern in-panel dialogue only). Caption voice should:

- open with a hook, not a summary --- the first ~125 characters are
  what shows before "more" truncates it in-feed;
- lead with curiosity/mystery, matching `Storytelling-DNA.md`'s
  "Curiosity Before Plot" and "Mystery Instead of Villains" principles
  --- never lead with the answer;
- state the science plainly and cautiously, matching the episode's own
  Learning Objective document --- don't overclaim beyond what the
  episode actually teaches;
- close with the episode's own closing line/tagline where one exists
  (e.g. S00E01's "Look closer before you decide.") rather than
  inventing a new one --- this creates continuity between the carousel
  images and the caption instead of repeating or contradicting them;
- end with a genuine, specific question, not a generic "thoughts?" ---
  matching `Storytelling-DNA.md`'s "Audience Participation" principle
  (the audience should feel like a third explorer, invited to notice
  and share, not just react).

### Caption Formula

```text
HOOK (1 line, stands alone before "more")
↓
blank line
↓
MYSTERY / DISCOVERY RECAP (2-3 sentences, in narrator voice)
↓
blank line
↓
SCIENCE TAKEAWAY (1-2 sentences, cautious, matches the Learning Objective doc)
↓
blank line
↓
EPISODE TAGLINE (the episode's own closing line, if one exists)
↓
blank line
↓
QUESTION (specific, inviting a real answer) + CTA emoji (e.g. 👇)
↓
blank line
↓
HASHTAGS (see below)
```

### Worked Example --- S00E01, "Why Is It Sleeping So Long?"

```text
She thought it was dead. 🌿

Sprout figured winter had finally taken this shrub — until she
looked closer and found something that changed her mind.

A brown branch doesn't always mean a dead plant. Sometimes winter
only damages part of it, while the rest is still alive underneath,
just waiting for spring to prove it.

Ranger Brooks and Sprout's lesson today: look closer before you
decide. 🔍

Ever almost pruned something that "looked dead"? Tell us in the
comments 👇

#LawnWithCare #YardRangers #LookCloserBeforeYouDecide
#BackyardNature #KidsLearning #NatureCuriosity
#GardeningWithKids #WinterInjury #AlbertaGardens #SpringInTheYard
```

Shorter alternative (when a punchier post fits better):

```text
"Is it dead?"

That was Sprout's first question. The answer wasn't that simple —
which is exactly why she had to look again. 🔍🌱

Look closer before you decide. Always.

#LawnWithCare #YardRangers #LookCloserBeforeYouDecide
```

## Hashtag Strategy

Mix four categories, roughly 8--12 tags total:

**Always include (branded):**
`#LawnWithCare` `#YardRangers`

**Episode tagline, when one exists:**
Turn the episode's closing line into a hashtag, no spaces --- e.g.
`#LookCloserBeforeYouDecide`. Reuse the same tag across all posts for
that episode (carousel + any Reels/Stories) so it functions as a
searchable thread.

**Broad discovery tags (rotate a few per post):**
`#BackyardNature` `#KidsLearning` `#NatureCuriosity`
`#GardeningWithKids` `#NatureForKids` `#ScienceForKids`

**Topic-specific (match the episode's actual Learning Objective ---
don't reuse S00E01's science tags on an unrelated episode):**
e.g. `#WinterInjury`, `#PollinatorGarden`, `#Composting` --- pull the
term from that episode's `02-Learning-Objective.md`.

**Regional (light touch, 1--2 max):**
`#AlbertaGardens` `#SpringInTheYard` `#CanadianGardens`

Do not front-load geography in the caption body itself --- keep
"Alberta"/"Canada" in hashtags and the bio, per the "Account" section
above.

## Publishing Workflow

```text
Assets/Final/S00E01-P01.png ... P08.png  (approved per 07-Production-Checklist.md)
↓
Export/verify each panel is still exactly 1080×1350, no re-compression artifacts
↓
Copy the approved set to Assets/Social/
↓
Draft caption using the formula above, referencing that episode's
  02-Learning-Objective.md and its closing line/tagline
↓
Select hashtags per the strategy above (branded + tagline + broad + topic + regional)
↓
Publish as an Instagram carousel, panels in P01→P08 order
↓
Pin the tagline hashtag's first use, if useful, for future cross-referencing
```

**Pre-publish checklist:**

- [ ] All panels are from `Assets/Final/`, not `Assets/Drafts/`.
- [ ] Panel order matches the script (P01 → P08).
- [ ] Caption follows the formula (hook / recap / science / tagline / question / hashtags).
- [ ] Science claim in the caption matches the episode's Learning
      Objective --- no overclaiming.
- [ ] Hashtags include both branded tags and the episode's tagline tag.
- [ ] Bio and profile picture are current (check once, not per post).
