# 🌱 Field Equipment

> "Tools do not make discoveries.
They help us notice what was already there."

---

# Purpose

This document defines every official recurring object used in the Lawn With Care Universe.

Field equipment is designed to support observation.

Never power.

Never authority.

Every object encourages curiosity.

---

# Design Philosophy

Every tool should feel:

Simple.

Timeless.

Durable.

Friendly.

Educational.

Authentic.

No equipment should resemble tactical, military or industrial gear.

Objects should invite children to explore.

Not intimidate them.

---

# Official Materials

Preferred materials:

Canvas

Wood

Leather

Brass

Matte aluminum

Glass

Natural fibers

Cotton

Recycled paper

Avoid glossy plastics whenever possible.

# Equipment Rules

Every object must:

Support observation.

Be scientifically believable.

Feel handmade or thoughtfully designed.

Be safe for children.

Encourage slow exploration.

Remain visually consistent.

---

# Things That Never Exist

Weapons.

Tactical gear.

Military backpacks.

Camouflage.

Drones as primary storytelling devices.

Lasers.

Futuristic scanners.

Magic detectors.

Talking gadgets.

Technology that replaces observation.

The characters must discover.

The equipment only assists.

---

# Wear and Aging

Equipment should age naturally.

Scratches.

Worn leather.

Faded paint.

Softened notebook edges.

Objects tell stories.

Perfection feels artificial.

---

# Ownership

Brooks cares for his tools.

Sprout personalizes hers.

Readers should recognize which object belongs to whom immediately.

---

# Visual Hierarchy

Most iconic objects:

1. Discovery Journal

2. Magnifying Glass

3. Campaign Hat

4. Discovery Band™

5. Field Kit

6. Discovery Frame™

7. Leaf Badge

---

# Educational Principle

Every tool exists to help someone ask a better question.

Never to provide instant answers.

---

# Official Statement

A Yard Ranger's greatest tool is attention.

Everything else simply helps focus it.

---

# Final Principle

The best equipment is the one that helps someone notice something they would have otherwise walked past.
