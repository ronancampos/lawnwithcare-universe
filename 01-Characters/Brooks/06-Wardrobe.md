# Ranger Brooks — Wardrobe

## Purpose

This document defines the canonical wardrobe system for Ranger Brooks.

It establishes:

- silhouette;
- clothing components;
- colors;
- brand placement;
- badge placement;
- footwear;
- acceptable variation;
- prohibited modifications.

Brooks' wardrobe must remain recognizable across illustration, comics, books, animation, educational materials and promotional content.

---

# 1. Wardrobe Philosophy

Brooks dresses like someone who actually spends time outdoors.

His clothing communicates:

- practicality;
- reliability;
- warmth;
- experience;
- care;
- approachability.

He should never appear:

- military;
- tactical;
- authoritarian;
- luxurious;
- fashionable for fashion's sake;
- superhero-like.

The wardrobe supports the character.

It must never overpower him.

---

# 2. Standard Field Uniform

Brooks' canonical standard field uniform consists of:

1. campaign hat;
2. forest-green field jacket;
3. light/cream undershirt;
4. practical outdoor trousers;
5. dark outdoor boots;
6. Lawn With Care `[L]` brand identifier;
7. Yard Ranger Canada badge;
8. optional field equipment when required by the story.

This is Brooks' default appearance.

---

# 3. Campaign Hat

The campaign hat is one of Brooks' strongest silhouette identifiers.

## Construction

The hat should have:

- broad structured brim;
- recognizable ranger-inspired crown;
- simple construction;
- clean silhouette.

## Primary Color

Forest Green:

`#0E2B1C`

## Hat Band

Gold / warm ochre:

`#C9A227`

The band should remain restrained and secondary to the overall hat silhouette.

---

# 4. Lawn With Care Hat Mark

The official Lawn With Care `[L]` leaf icon may appear centered on the front of the campaign hat.

It must use the **official supplied artwork**.

The icon must not be:

- redrawn;
- reconstructed;
- distorted;
- stylized;
- simplified;
- mirrored;
- reinterpreted by generative AI.

Cropping excess transparent or empty border space is permitted.

The internal logo artwork must remain unchanged.

The icon should remain small enough that Brooks' hat still reads primarily as a ranger hat rather than promotional merchandise.

---

# 5. Field Jacket

Brooks wears a practical outdoor field jacket.

## Primary Color

Forest Green:

`#0E2B1C`

The jacket should feel:

- durable;
- comfortable;
- functional;
- slightly structured;
- appropriate for outdoor work.

Avoid excessive seams, straps, armor-like panels or tactical details.

---

# 6. Jacket Construction

Preferred features include:

- simple collar;
- front opening;
- practical chest pockets;
- functional side pockets;
- clean cuffs;
- restrained stitching.

The jacket may show subtle signs of real outdoor use but should never appear dirty or neglected.

---

# 7. Lawn With Care Jacket Mark

A small official Lawn With Care `[L]` icon may appear on Brooks' jacket.

Preferred canonical placement:

**Brooks' left chest — viewer's right when facing Brooks.**

The application should resemble a professional embroidered brand identifier.

It must remain:

- small;
- clean;
- readable;
- proportional.

Do not place a large Lawn With Care logo across Brooks' torso.

---

# 8. Yard Ranger Canada Badge

Brooks carries a separate Yard Ranger badge.

This badge is **not** the Lawn With Care corporate logo.

Its purpose is to communicate:

- Yard Ranger identity;
- Brooks' Canadian context;
- stewardship;
- belonging to the Yard Ranger world.

## Canonical Visual Direction

The badge uses:

- white or very light background;
- red maple leaf;
- restrained border;
- simple readable construction.

The maple leaf provides a direct visual reference to Canada.

---

# 9. Symbol Hierarchy

Brooks carries two distinct identifiers.

## Lawn With Care `[L]`

Represents:

**Brand affiliation**

Primary applications:

- campaign hat;
- jacket.

## Red Maple Leaf Yard Ranger Badge

Represents:

**Yard Ranger identity + Canadian context**

Primary application:

- chest badge.

The two symbols must remain visually and conceptually distinct.

---

# 10. Brand Restraint

Brooks is a character first.

He is not a walking advertisement.

Do not duplicate Lawn With Care branding unnecessarily.

Avoid placing `[L]` icons simultaneously on:

- sleeves;
- both chest sides;
- trousers;
- belt;
- boots;
- gloves;
- every prop;
- every piece of equipment.

Brand presence should feel integrated naturally into the universe.

---

# 11. Undershirt

A simple light undershirt may be visible beneath the jacket.

Preferred tone:

Cream:

`#F5F4EE`

It should remain visually quiet.

No additional graphics or slogans are required.

---

# 12. Trousers

Brooks wears practical outdoor trousers.

Preferred direction:

- earthy green / olive;
- relaxed functional cut;
- enough mobility for kneeling and field observation;
- subtle utility pockets.

The trousers should not resemble military combat pants.

Avoid excessive cargo pockets or tactical detailing.

---

# 13. Boots

Brooks wears sturdy outdoor boots.

Primary color:

`#111111`

or a closely controlled very dark neutral.

The boots should communicate:

- stability;
- durability;
- outdoor use;
- comfort.

They should not resemble combat boots.

---

# 14. Silhouette

Brooks must remain recognizable from silhouette alone.

Key wardrobe silhouette elements:

- campaign hat;
- field jacket;
- practical trousers;
- sturdy boots.

Do not introduce clothing that significantly alters this silhouette without an approved design revision.

---

# 15. Seasonal Variation

Brooks may use seasonal wardrobe adaptations when environmentally appropriate.

Examples include:

- light rain shell;
- warmer field layer;
- gloves;
- winter outerwear;
- sun-protection layer.

Seasonal variations must preserve:

- Brooks' silhouette;
- official color relationships;
- Yard Ranger identity;
- restrained Lawn With Care branding.

Seasonal clothing is an adaptation of Brooks.

It is not a redesign.

---

# 16. Story-Specific Variation

Minor wardrobe variation is allowed when required by:

- weather;
- field activity;
- safety;
- educational context;
- location.

Any variation must remain immediately recognizable as Brooks.

---

# 17. Prohibited Wardrobe Directions

Do not depict Brooks wearing:

- camouflage;
- military uniforms;
- police-style uniforms;
- tactical vests;
- body armor;
- superhero costumes;
- formal business suits as standard wardrobe;
- excessive corporate branding;
- fantasy armor;
- futuristic combat clothing.

Brooks is a Yard Ranger and naturalist mentor.

---

# 18. Logo Production Rule

Generative AI must not be trusted to reproduce the official Lawn With Care icon exactly.

For final branded production, preferred workflow is:

1. generate or illustrate Brooks with the correct logo placement reserved;
2. use the official Lawn With Care source asset;
3. composite the official icon into the final artwork;
4. verify proportions and placement;
5. export the final production asset.

AI-generated approximations may be acceptable during concept development but are not authoritative corporate artwork.

Brooks' standard field uniform is canonical.

Changes affecting:

- primary silhouette;
- campaign hat;
- primary jacket;
- official branding;
- badge identity;
- core color relationships

require an explicit canon revision.

Minor functional and seasonal adaptations do not require a redesign as long as they comply with this document.
