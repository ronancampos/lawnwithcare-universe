# Brooks

# Design Rules

> "Consistency is invisible until it is broken."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the permanent visual rules for Brooks.

These are implementation rules.

They complement the Visual DNA.

Visual DNA explains what Brooks represents.

Design Rules explain exactly how he must always be illustrated.

Whenever uncertainty exists, this document takes precedence over artistic preference.

---

# Core Design Philosophy

Every illustration should answer one question:

**"Would a child immediately trust Brooks?"**

If the answer is no, the design should be revised.

Brooks should never appear intimidating.

---

# Visual Priorities

Whenever Brooks appears, the visual hierarchy should reinforce:

1. Face
2. Campaign Hat
3. Yard Ranger Canada Badge
4. Hands
5. Notebook
6. Field Kit
7. Lawn With Care brand identifier

The audience should naturally read the character in this order.

---

# Silhouette Rules

Brooks must always remain recognizable by silhouette.

Essential silhouette elements:

• Campaign hat

• Straight posture

• Relaxed shoulders

• Rounded boots

• Notebook

• Ranger jacket

These six elements define the character.

Never redesign them.

---

# Campaign Hat

The campaign hat is Brooks' primary visual identifier.

It must always:

Remain forest green (#0E2B1C)

Have the gold band (#C9A227)

Keep the same proportions

Have a slightly rounded crown

Maintain the same brim width

Never appear tactical.

Never become cowboy-like.

Never become military.

Never become oversized.

Never become fashionable.

It is functional.

Timeless.

Professional.

---

# Badge

# Yard Ranger Canada Badge

The official Yard Ranger Canada badge is a permanent component of Brooks' standard field identity.

It is visually distinct from the Lawn With Care corporate brand.

Canonical direction:

White or light background.

Red maple leaf.

Restrained border treatment.

Clean, simple construction.

Placement:

Brooks' chest, according to the approved wardrobe and Master Visual Reference.

The badge represents:

**Yard Ranger identity + Canadian context.**

It must never be replaced by the Lawn With Care `[L]` icon.

It should remain visible whenever the wardrobe and camera angle reasonably permit it.

Do not redesign the badge without an approved canon revision.

---

# Lawn With Care Brand Identifier

The official Lawn With Care `[L]` leaf icon is Brooks' corporate brand identifier.

It represents:

**Lawn With Care brand affiliation.**

Approved primary applications:

Campaign hat.

Field jacket.

Only official Lawn With Care source artwork may be used in final branded production.

The icon must never be:

Redrawn.

Reconstructed.

Mirrored.

Distorted.

Simplified.

Reinterpreted.

Replaced by an AI-generated approximation.

Cropping excess transparent or empty border space is permitted.

The internal artwork must remain unchanged.

Branding must remain restrained.

Brooks is a character first, not a walking advertisement.

# Hands

Hands are one of Brooks' teaching tools.

They should remain slightly larger than realistic.

Used to:

Point gently.

Hold the notebook.

Gesture naturally.

Invite observation.

Never point aggressively.

Never use clenched fists unless absolutely required by the story.

Brooks should always appear approachable.

Rounded facial structure.

Soft jawline.

Kind eyes.

Relaxed smile.

Harsh cheekbones.

Deep wrinkles.

Extreme expressions.

Angular features.

---

# Eyes

Warm.

Curious.

Focused.

# Posture

Always upright.

Relaxed.

Balanced.

Confident.

Never arrogant.

Never slouched.

---

# Gesture Rules

Preferred gestures:

Open palm.

Pointing with one finger.

Holding notebook.

Offering objects.

Kneeling.

Looking closely.

Avoid:

Crossed arms while teaching.

Hands in pockets during explanations.

Pointing at people.

Aggressive movements.

---

# Walking Style

Steady pace.

Long relaxed strides.

Observant.

He often looks around while walking.

Never rushes without narrative reason.

---

# Kneeling

Whenever Brooks examines nature closely:

He bends one knee.

Keeps balance.

Remains comfortable.

Never squats awkwardly.

This pose should become iconic.

---

# Relationship with Scale

Nature should always feel important.

Do not exaggerate Brooks' size.

Trees should feel tall.

Grass should occasionally reach knee height.

Flowers may occupy significant portions of the frame.

Nature shares protagonism.

---

# Camera Rules

Preferred:

Eye level.

Three-quarter view.

Ground perspective.

Medium close-up.

Over-the-shoulder during investigations.

Avoid:

Hero shots.

Extreme low angles.

Dutch angles.

Action movie framing.

---

# Lighting Rules

Preferred:

Overcast.

Soft forest light.

Avoid:

Neon lighting.

Harsh studio lighting.

High contrast noir.

Colored dramatic spotlights.

# AI Consistency Rules

Every generated image must preserve:

Same face.

Same proportions.

Same hat.

Same badge.

Same palette.

Same posture language.

Same emotional tone.

Same educational purpose.

AI-generated artwork must be cross-checked against:

`Assets/Model-Sheets/Brooks-Master-Model-Sheet-v1.0.png`

The Master Model Sheet is the approved visual reference for Brooks v1.0.

Generative AI is not authoritative for corporate artwork.

When exact Lawn With Care branding is required, the official source logo/icon should be composited into the final artwork rather than relying on AI reproduction.

An AI-generated variation does not become canon merely because it appears in an illustration.

# Design Checklist

Before approving any artwork ask:

✓ Is Brooks immediately recognizable?

✓ Is the campaign hat correct?

✓ Is the badge correct?

✓ Is the posture welcoming?

✓ Is the palette accurate?

✓ Does he feel like a mentor?

✓ Does he look ready to investigate?

If any answer is "No", revise the artwork.

---

# Design Statement

Brooks should never look like an action hero.

He should look like someone every child hopes to meet during a walk in nature.

---

# Official Design Principle

Design should communicate trust before expertise.

Trust creates curiosity.

Curiosity creates discovery.
