# Ranger Brooks — Official Model Sheet

> "Consistency creates trust."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official visual construction of Ranger Brooks.

It exists to guarantee that Brooks remains recognizable regardless of:

- artist
- AI model
- illustration style
- camera angle
- medium

This document takes precedence over individual illustrations.

If an illustration conflicts with this specification, the specification is correct.

---

# Character Role

Brooks is the adult Yard Ranger.

He represents:

- patience
- observation
- curiosity
- calm leadership
- scientific thinking
- kindness

His design must communicate those qualities before he speaks.

---

# Design Philosophy

Brooks should immediately communicate:

"I feel safe exploring nature with this person."

He is approachable.

Competent.

Warm.

Never intimidating.

---

# Shape Language

Primary shapes:

Rounded rectangles.

Soft curves.

Gentle silhouettes.

Avoid:

Sharp angles.

Aggressive shapes.

Military proportions.

Superhero anatomy.

---

# Body Proportions

Height

7.25 heads

Build

Athletic but realistic.

Strong enough to work outdoors.

Never exaggerated.

Shoulders

Moderately broad.

Waist

Natural.

Hands

Slightly larger than realistic.

Important for expressive gestures.

Feet

Large enough to feel stable.

---

# Face

Expression should naturally appear calm.

Default emotion:

Gentle curiosity.

Eyes

Medium size.

Friendly.

Attentive.

Eyebrows

Expressive.

Used more than mouth for subtle emotions.

Smile

Usually small.

Authentic.

Never exaggerated.

---

# Hair

No visible hair.

The campaign hat remains on in almost every appearance.

---

# Hat

Official campaign ranger hat.

Forest Green (#0E2B1C).

Gold hat band.

Consistent proportions in every drawing.

Never redesigned.

---

# Uniform

Official colors only.

Forest Green

Fresh Green

Cream

Gold accents

Black boots

No camouflage.

No tactical appearance.

No unnecessary accessories.

---

# Badge

Official Yard Ranger badge.

Always positioned consistently.

Never oversized.

Always readable at close range.

---

# Backpack

Optional.

Appears during exploration stories.

Never oversized.

Should feel practical.

---

# Field Notebook

Core prop.

Frequently appears.

Should look worn through use.

Represents observation rather than authority.

---

# Pencil

Traditional wooden pencil.

Frequently held.

Small but iconic.

---

# Boots

Black.

Outdoor style.

Clean but realistically used.

---

# Standing Pose

Relaxed.

Weight evenly distributed.

Should communicate availability.

Not command.

---

# Walking Pose

Purposeful.

Calm.

Never heroic.

---

# Kneeling Pose

One knee close to the ground.

Frequently used while observing nature.

One of Brooks' signature poses.

---

# Observation Pose

Hands behind back

or

Notebook in one hand.

Eyes focused.

Body leaning slightly forward.

---

# Gesture Language

Brooks rarely points dramatically.

Instead he:

opens his hand,

leans closer,

invites others to observe.
