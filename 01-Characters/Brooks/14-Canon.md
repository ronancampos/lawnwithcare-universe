# Brooks

# Canon

> "Consistency creates trust.
Trust creates timeless characters."

---

# Purpose

This document defines Brooks' immutable canon.

These rules cannot be broken without an official franchise decision.

If a story contradicts this document, the story is not considered canon.

---

# Character Purpose

Brooks exists to inspire curiosity.

He is not the protagonist because he is the smartest.

He is the protagonist because he helps others become curious.

---

# Identity

Brooks is always:

Patient.

Observant.

Respectful.

Kind.

Calm.

Curious.

Scientific.

Reliable.

Optimistic.

Encouraging.

---

Brooks is never:

Arrogant.

Cruel.

Impulsive.

Sarcastic toward children.

Fear-driven.

Careless.

Dismissive.

Cynical.

Self-centered.

Aggressive.

---

# Curiosity

Curiosity is Brooks' defining characteristic.

Whenever he encounters something unknown, his first reaction is:

Observe.

Not judge.

Not assume.

Not fear.

Observe.

---

# Teaching

Brooks never lectures.

He guides.

He asks questions.

He creates opportunities for discovery.

He celebrates learning.

He does not perform.

He teaches.

---

# Scientific Method

Brooks always follows the same reasoning process.

Observe.

Question.

Collect evidence.

Compare.

Think.

Conclude.

Share.

He never jumps directly to conclusions.

---

# Relationship with Children

Brooks treats children as capable thinkers.

He never:

talks down

ridicules

embarrasses

ignores

dismisses

their ideas.

Every question deserves respect.

---

# Relationship with Sprout

Brooks never steals Sprout's discoveries.

He creates the conditions for her to find answers herself.

He is her mentor.

Not her replacement.

---

# Relationship with Nature

Nature is never an obstacle.

Never an enemy.

Never merely scenery.

Nature is Brooks' greatest teacher.

He treats every living organism with respect.

---

# Leadership

Brooks leads through example.

Never through authority.

He walks first.

Listens first.

Observes first.

Speaks last.

---

# Decision Making

Evidence comes before opinion.

Observation comes before explanation.

Respect comes before action.

---

# Communication

Brooks speaks calmly.

He rarely interrupts.

He uses clear language.

He prefers questions over speeches.

He never uses complicated words simply to sound intelligent.

---

# Emotional Control

Brooks experiences emotions deeply.

He simply manages them well.

He remains calm under pressure.

He is allowed to worry.

He is never allowed to panic.

---

# Humor

Brooks enjoys gentle humor.

His jokes are based on:

Observation.

Unexpected discoveries.

Self-awareness.

Nature's surprises.

He never laughs at people.

He laughs with people.

Often, he laughs at himself.

---

# Courage

Brooks is brave.

Not because he is fearless.

Because curiosity is stronger than fear.

---

# Failure

Brooks is allowed to make mistakes.

He is never embarrassed by learning.

When evidence changes...

he changes his mind.

---

# Signature Habits

Brooks always:

Carries his notebook.

Carries a pencil.

Observes before explaining.

Writes observations immediately.

Encourages others to look first.

Treats discoveries with enthusiasm.

Protects living organisms.

---

# Signature Questions

Recurring questions include:

"What do you notice?"

"What do you think happened here?"

"What makes you say that?"

"What evidence do we have?"

"What else could explain this?"

Questions are Brooks' primary teaching tool.

---

# Signature Behaviors

Adjusts his ranger hat before investigations.

Kneels to observe small things.

Smiles quietly after discoveries.

Looks carefully before speaking.

Invites others closer instead of stepping forward himself.

---

# Visual Identity

Brooks is never shown without:

Campaign Hat

or

a narrative reason explaining its absence.

The hat is part of his identity.

---

The official ranger badge is always present.

The notebook is almost always present.

The Discovery Frame appears whenever focused observation is encouraged.

---

# Uniform

The ranger uniform evolves only through officially documented wardrobe updates.

Core identity never changes.

Forest green.

Gold accents.

Cream badge.

Black boots.

Official insignia.

---

# Discovery

Brooks never pretends to know everything.

He is comfortable saying:

"I don't know yet."

This sentence is one of his greatest strengths.

---

# Philosophy

People protect what they understand.

People understand what they observe.

Observation creates care.

This philosophy guides every decision Brooks makes.

---

# What Brooks Never Does

Never lies to children.

Never invents scientific explanations.

Never humiliates someone for making mistakes.

Never encourages harming animals.

Never damages nature unnecessarily.

Never ignores evidence.

Never acts recklessly.

Never uses fear as motivation.

Never ridicules curiosity.

Never gives up on teaching.

---

# What Brooks Always Does

Encourages questions.

Celebrates curiosity.

Protects living things.

Observes carefully.

Keeps learning.

Respects uncertainty.

Listens first.

Shares discoveries.

Works as a team.

Remains hopeful.

---

# Emotional Mission

Whenever Brooks appears, the audience should feel:

Safe.

Curious.

Inspired.

Welcome.

Capable of learning.

Closer to nature.

---

# Story Function

Brooks is not the hero because he solves mysteries.

He is the hero because he teaches others how to solve them.

---

# Canon Integrity

Future stories may expand Brooks.

They may never contradict:

His values.

His philosophy.

His relationship with nature.

His teaching style.

His emotional tone.

His visual identity.

---

# Canon Test

Before approving any story ask:

Does Brooks encourage curiosity?

Does Brooks respect evidence?

Does Brooks treat children as equals?

Does Brooks remain humble?

Does Brooks inspire observation?

If any answer is "No",

the story is not canon.

---

# Character Statement

Brooks reminds us that wisdom does not begin with knowing.

It begins with noticing.

---

# Official Canon Principle

Brooks never seeks to be the smartest person in the story.

He seeks to help others discover how capable they already are.
