# Purpose

It establishes the immutable identity of the character before any visual, dialogue or animation decisions are made.

Everything related to Brooks must be consistent with this document.

---

# Character Overview

Brooks is the adult protagonist of the Lawn With Care Universe.

He serves as mentor, guide and field naturalist for the Yard Rangers.

Rather than solving problems for others, Brooks teaches children—and the audience—how to observe, investigate and understand the natural world for themselves.

He believes curiosity is humanity's greatest tool.

# Core Concept

Brooks represents the adult every curious child hopes to meet.

He is knowledgeable without being intimidating.

Confident without arrogance.

Patient without being passive.

Strong without appearing aggressive.

His greatest talent is helping others discover answers themselves.

# Mission

# Life Philosophy

Nature is not something humans own.

Humans are part of nature.

Every living thing deserves attention.

Questions are more valuable than assumptions.

Observation always comes before explanation.

---

# Personality

Brooks is calm by nature.

He rarely raises his voice.

He enjoys silence.

He thinks before speaking.

He is naturally optimistic without ignoring reality.

His confidence comes from experience—not ego.

He celebrates curiosity more than correct answers.

---

# Emotional Intelligence

Brooks reads emotions well.

He notices when someone is discouraged.

He adapts his teaching style to the learner.

He knows when to explain.

He knows when to remain silent.

He understands that wonder cannot be forced.

---

# Strengths

Patient listener.

Excellent observer.

Scientific reasoning.

Problem solving.

Environmental knowledge.

Empathy.

Leadership through example.

Calm under pressure.

Encouraging mentor.

Reliable decision maker.

---

# Weaknesses

Can become deeply absorbed in observations.

Sometimes forgets time while investigating nature.

Prefers exploration over paperwork.

May underestimate how inspiring he is to others.

Occasionally carries too much responsibility himself.

---

# Fears

That future generations stop noticing nature.

Losing biodiversity.

Teaching without inspiring curiosity.

People believing they already know everything.

---

# Internal Conflict

Brooks constantly asks himself:

"Am I explaining too much?"

He wants children to discover answers themselves.

Finding that balance is his lifelong challenge.

---

# External Conflict

Most challenges come from misunderstanding nature rather than fighting villains.

Weather.

Changing seasons.

Human assumptions.

Environmental mysteries.

Lack of observation.

---

# Leadership Style

Lead by example.

Never by authority.

He walks first.

Observes first.

Listens first.

Speaks last.

People follow Brooks because they trust him.

Never because they fear him.

---

# Teaching Philosophy

Never lecture.

Never embarrass.

Never show off knowledge.

Instead:

Ask.

Observe.

Compare.

Investigate.

Understand.

Celebrate.

---

# Curiosity Profile

Brooks believes curiosity is a skill.

It can be practiced.

Improved.

Shared.

He encourages questions even when he does not know the answer.

"I don't know yet."

is a perfectly acceptable response.

---

# Relationship with Nature

Nature is Brooks' oldest teacher.

He treats every ecosystem with respect.

He never speaks about nature as a resource first.

He speaks about it as a living community.

---

# Relationship with Children

Brooks never talks down to children.

He simplifies language.

Never ideas.

He believes children are fully capable of scientific thinking.

---

# Relationship with Sprout

Sprout constantly surprises Brooks.

She notices details he occasionally overlooks.

Their partnership is based on mutual respect.

Brooks teaches methods.

Sprout teaches perspective.

Together they become better observers.

---

# Relationship with Animals

Animals are never "cute props."

Each has a purpose.

Brooks encourages understanding before interaction.

Observe first.

Touch only when appropriate.

Respect always.
