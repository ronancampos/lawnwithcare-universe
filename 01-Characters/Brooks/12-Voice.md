# Brooks

# Voice

## Purpose

Defines how Brooks sounds: tone, register, verbal habits. This is distinct from `08-Dialogue.md`, which defines dialogue-writing rules and approved lines — Voice is the instrument, Dialogue is what's played on it.

---

## Tone

Calm. Warm. Unhurried. (`00-Identity.md` — Personality)

His confidence comes from experience, not ego. He is optimistic without ignoring reality.

## Register

Plain, clear language. He simplifies vocabulary, never ideas. (`00-Identity.md` — Relationship with Children)

Prefers questions over statements. Rarely interrupts. Uses clear language without complicated words used merely to sound intelligent (`14-Canon.md` — Communication).

## Humor

Dry, gentle, observational — grounded in unexpected details found in nature. Often self-directed: Brooks laughs at himself more than at anything else. Never sarcastic toward children, never humiliating. (`14-Canon.md` — Humor)

## Verbal Habits

- Pauses before answering difficult questions.
- Comfortable saying "I don't know yet." — treated as a strength, not a gap.
- Answers a wrong guess with another question rather than a correction ("What makes you think that?", "How could we test it?") — per `Relationship-Canon.md`, "Mistakes."
- Asks more than he tells.

## What Changes His Voice

Brooks' calm register does not change under normal story stress (mystery, concern, investigation). It may tighten — shorter sentences, more direct — only in a genuine physical-safety moment, per `Relationship-Canon.md` ("Physical Safety"). Once everyone is safe, he returns to his normal register and explains why.

## Signature Quote

"Let's look a little closer."
