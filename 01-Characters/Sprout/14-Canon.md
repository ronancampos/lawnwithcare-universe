# Sprout

# Canon

> "Curiosity is not childish.
It is humanity's oldest way of learning."

---

# Purpose

This document defines Sprout's immutable canon.

These principles may only be changed through an official franchise decision.

Every comic, illustration, animation, book and AI-generated story must remain consistent with these rules.

---

# Character Purpose

Sprout exists to remind the audience that wonder is a way of seeing.

She is not the smartest character.

She is the one who notices what everyone else overlooked.

---

# Identity

Sprout is always:

Curious.

Kind.

Patient.

Respectful.

Optimistic.

Observant.

Gentle.

Creative.

Empathetic.

Hopeful.

---

Sprout is never:

Mean.

Cruel.

Arrogant.

Competitive.

Impulsive.

Disrespectful.

Selfish.

Fear-driven.

Sarcastic.

Attention-seeking.

---

# Curiosity

Curiosity defines every decision Sprout makes.

Whenever something unexpected appears, she always chooses to:

Observe.

Ask.

Think.

Explore.

She never judges before looking carefully.

---

# Relationship with Brooks

Brooks is her mentor.

He never replaces her discoveries.

Sprout never depends on Brooks to think.

She depends on Brooks to organize her thinking.

Their relationship is collaborative.

Never hierarchical.

---

# Relationship with Nature

Nature is never scenery.

Nature is alive.

Sprout treats every plant and animal as part of a much larger story.

She never collects living organisms unnecessarily.

She observes before interacting.

---

# Relationship with Animals

Sprout loves animals.

She never assumes they need rescuing.

She understands that observing respectfully is often the greatest act of care.

---

# Relationship with Children

Sprout represents every child.

She never behaves as an unrealistic genius.

Her intelligence comes from observation.

Not extraordinary knowledge.

Children should think:

"That could be me."

---

# Scientific Thinking

Sprout asks questions before seeking answers.

She enjoys making predictions.

She learns to test ideas.

She celebrates changing her mind when evidence changes.

She never invents facts.

---

# Discovery

Sprout values discovering over being right.

She enjoys the process more than the conclusion.

Finding evidence is exciting.

Learning is exciting.

Sharing discoveries is exciting.

---

# Emotional Intelligence

Sprout notices feelings quickly.

She comforts others naturally.

She apologizes sincerely when necessary.

She forgives easily.

She celebrates other people's discoveries.

---

# Communication

Sprout speaks simply.

Clearly.

Honestly.

She asks many questions.

She rarely interrupts.

She listens carefully.

She respects silence during observations.

---

# Humor

Her humor comes naturally.

She is unintentionally funny.

She notices unusual connections.

She makes innocent observations that surprise Brooks.

She never makes jokes to embarrass anyone.

---

# Courage

Sprout is brave because curiosity is stronger than fear.

She may become nervous.

She never stops asking questions.

---

# Failure

Sprout is allowed to make mistakes.

Mistakes become discoveries.

She never feels ashamed of learning.

She never pretends to know something she doesn't.

---

# Signature Habits

Sprout always:

Carries her magnifying glass.

Observes before touching.

Looks very closely.

Collects interesting leaves only when appropriate.

Looks upward whenever she hears birds.

Smiles after noticing patterns.

Shares discoveries immediately.

---

# Signature Questions

Recurring questions include:

"What do you notice?"

"Why do you think that happened?"

"What if we look a little closer?"

"Has it always been like this?"

"I wonder..."

These questions define her curiosity.

---

# Signature Behaviors

Tilts her head.

Squats instead of bending.

Looks through her magnifying glass.

Touches plants gently.

Holds discoveries carefully.

Pauses when she notices something unusual.

Often whispers:

"Wait..."

before pointing at an important clue.

---

# Discovery Philosophy

Every discovery belongs to everyone.

Sprout never competes.

She immediately shares interesting findings.

She believes discoveries become more meaningful when experienced together.

---

# Learning

Sprout never studies to impress.

She learns because learning is joyful.

Her excitement comes from understanding.

Not achievement.

---

# Wonder

Wonder is her defining emotional state.

Wonder is not surprise.

Wonder is quiet.

Focused.

Respectful.

Deep.

Sprout spends more time observing than reacting.

---

# Visual Identity

Sprout is never shown without:

Her curly blonde hair.

Her magnifying glass.

Her Discovery Band.

Or a narrative reason explaining their absence.

These elements define her silhouette.

---

# Discovery Band

The Discovery Band is never a symbol of authority.

It represents learning.

Badges celebrate discoveries.

Not rank.

Badges accumulate throughout the series.

Existing badges are never removed.

---

# What Sprout Never Does

Never laughs at curiosity.

Never hurts living things intentionally.

Never invents scientific explanations.

Never lies to Brooks.

Never ignores evidence.

Never ridicules mistakes.

Never gives up because something is difficult.

Never stops asking questions.

---

# What Sprout Always Does

Observe first.

Ask questions.

Respect nature.

Share discoveries.

Listen carefully.

Learn continuously.

Encourage others.

Protect living things.

Celebrate curiosity.

Remain hopeful.

---

# Emotional Mission

Whenever Sprout appears, the audience should feel:

Curious.

Safe.

Inspired.

Capable.

Connected to nature.

Excited to explore.

---

# Story Function

Sprout does not solve mysteries because she knows the answers.

She solves mysteries because she notices the clues.

---

# Canon Integrity

Future stories may expand Sprout.

They may never contradict:

Her optimism.

Her curiosity.

Her kindness.

Her respect for nature.

Her relationship with Brooks.

Her emotional warmth.

Her visual identity.

---

# Canon Test

Before approving any story ask:

Does Sprout encourage curiosity?

Does Sprout respect nature?

Does Sprout ask genuine questions?

Does Sprout remain kind?

Does Sprout inspire observation?

If any answer is "No",

the story is not canon.

---

# Character Statement

Sprout reminds us that the world has never stopped being magical.

Sometimes we simply stop paying attention.

---

# Official Canon Principle

Sprout never tries to become the smartest person in the story.

She simply refuses to stop wondering.
