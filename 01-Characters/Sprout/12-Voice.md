# Sprout

# Voice

## Purpose

Defines how Sprout sounds: tone, register, verbal habits. This is distinct from `08-Dialogue.md`, which defines dialogue-writing rules and approved lines — Voice is the instrument, Dialogue is what's played on it.

---

## Tone

Naturally curious. Gentle. Optimistic. Patient when observing; energetic when discovering — but her energy comes from fascination, not hyperactivity. She speaks softly when something captures her attention. (`00-Identity.md` — Personality)

## Register

Warm, simple, honest, sincere. Asks many questions. Rarely interrupts. Comfortable with — even enjoys — silence during observation. (`14-Canon.md` — Communication)

## Humor

Unintentional. Comes from innocent observations and unexpected connections. She never tries to be funny — she simply says what she genuinely thinks, and that honesty is often what makes it funny. (`00-Identity.md` / `14-Canon.md` — Humor)

## Verbal Habits

- Often says "Wait..." before pointing out something interesting; sometimes whispers it before an important clue. (`00-Identity.md` — Signature Behaviors)
- Tilts her head when thinking; the verbal equivalent is a short pause before a question, not a rushed one.
- Shares discoveries immediately rather than saving them up — her dialogue should reflect real-time noticing, not retrospective summary.

## What Changes Her Voice

Sprout's warmth and curiosity hold steady across the story. The one documented shift is her Internal Conflict (`00-Identity.md`): she may briefly worry aloud that she's asking "too many questions" — a rare, quiet register change that Brooks' reassurance resolves. This is the only canon-sanctioned dip in her otherwise consistent voice.

## Signature Quote

"The smallest questions often lead to the biggest discoveries." (`00-Identity.md`, opening quote)
