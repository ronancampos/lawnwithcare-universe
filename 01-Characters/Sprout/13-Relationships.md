## Sprout + Brooks

Primary relationship. See `Relationship-Canon.md` in full.

One-line summary: Brooks is her mentor and her learning partner. She trusts him completely; he never replaces her discoveries, only organizes her thinking.

## Sprout + Nature

Governed by `14-Canon.md` ("Relationship with Nature") and `00-Identity.md` ("Relationship with Nature"). Nature is never scenery — it is her playground, classroom and friend. She never collects living organisms unnecessarily and observes before interacting.

## Sprout + Animals

Governed by `14-Canon.md` ("Relationship with Animals") and `00-Identity.md` ("Relationship with Animals"). She never assumes an animal needs rescuing; she observes first and asks Brooks before interfering. Every animal is "living its own story."

## Sprout + Children (audience representation)

Governed by `14-Canon.md` ("Relationship with Children") and `00-Identity.md` ("Relationship with Children"). She represents every curious child — not a genius, simply more attentive. The audience should think "that could be me," not "I could never be that smart."
