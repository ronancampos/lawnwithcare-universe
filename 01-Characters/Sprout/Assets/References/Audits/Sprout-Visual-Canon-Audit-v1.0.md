# Sprout Visual Canon Audit v1.0

> "Generated variations may explore the character. They may never
> redefine the character."

## Purpose

This audit consolidates the current visual canon for Sprout and
distinguishes CANON / LOCKED elements, approved production references,
controlled systems, optional/story-specific elements, and AI-generated
variations that are not automatically canon.

No element becomes canon merely because it appears in an AI-generated
sheet.

## Authority Hierarchy

When visual references conflict:

1.  Approved written Sprout canon and design documents
2.  `Sprout-Master-Model-Sheet-v1.0.png`
3.  Official Lawn With Care brand assets
4.  Approved specialized Sprout sheets
5.  Individual AI-generated artwork

The Master Model Sheet is the primary authority for Sprout's physical
appearance, proportions, silhouette and core outfit. Specialized sheets
may not redesign the character.

## Official Lawn With Care Brand Authority

Official Lawn With Care assets are stored under `00-Franchise/Brand/`.

Official logo files must be used exactly as supplied. AI-generated
reproductions are not authoritative. Do not redraw, reconstruct, add
leaves, alter framing, recolor, distort, retype, simplify or reinterpret
them. Cropping excess empty border is permitted; changing internal
artwork is not.

## Character Identity --- CANON / LOCKED

-   Visual age: approximately **9 years old**
-   Approximate height: **130--135 cm**
-   Golden blonde, naturally curly hair with substantial visible volume
-   Bright light-green eyes
-   Subtle natural freckles
-   Green fabric headband
-   Warm, youthful, approachable facial proportions

Avoid adult facial proportions, glamour styling, exaggerated infant
proportions or aggressive presentation.

## Core Outfit --- CANON / LOCKED

Sprout's everyday explorer outfit consists of:

-   cream short-sleeve field shirt;
-   olive-green shorts;
-   practical belt;
-   brown outdoor boots;
-   cream/light socks;
-   green headband;
-   Discovery Band.

The outfit remains practical, comfortable, age-appropriate,
nature-oriented and non-tactical.

## Discovery Band --- CANON / LOCKED SYSTEM

The Discovery Band is one of Sprout's principal visual and narrative
identifiers. It represents learning, progress, discovery, care,
completed missions and growing responsibility.

Approved production direction:

-   durable green canvas;
-   natural textile finish;
-   metal progress ring;
-   designated badge positions;
-   restrained gold/brass hardware.

It must not become military, ceremonial, luxurious, futuristic or
excessively decorative.

### Progress Ring --- CONTROLLED

The progress ring is part of the Discovery Band system. Exact
progression stages, symbols and mechanics require system/narrative
approval. AI-generated examples are references only until formally
documented.

### Badge System --- CONTROLLED / NOT FULLY CANONIZED

Badges may represent nature, observation, science, care, creativity,
teamwork and missions. Individual badges appearing in generated sheets
are **not automatically canon**.

A badge becomes canon only when explicitly added to the approved badge
system. Episode artwork must not invent permanent new badges without
approval.

## Props

### Magnifying Loupe --- CANON / SIGNATURE PROP

Sprout's primary discovery tool. It reinforces the behavior: **Look
closer.**

### Field Notebook --- APPROVED CORE PROP

Used for observations, questions, sketches and discoveries.

### Pencil --- APPROVED CORE PROP

Simple, practical and age-appropriate.

### Sample Containers --- CONTROLLED / STORY-SPECIFIC

May appear when scientifically and narratively appropriate. They must
not encourage unnecessary harm to living organisms.

### Optional / Story-Specific Props

Generated references may propose field pack, clipboard, field guide,
hand trowel, ruler, binoculars, compass, sample pouch, slide viewer or
water bottle. Their appearance in a sheet does not make them permanent
equipment.

## Seasonal Wardrobe --- OPTIONAL / STORY-SPECIFIC

Climate-appropriate variations may exist for cool weather, rain, summer,
field work and winter. They must preserve Sprout's recognizable identity
and do not replace the canonical everyday outfit.

## Color Identity --- APPROVED PRODUCTION REFERENCE

Sprout's color language is warm, natural, optimistic and readable:

-   golden blonde hair;
-   light-green eyes;
-   warm natural skin;
-   cream shirt;
-   olive-green shorts;
-   green headband;
-   brown boots;
-   forest-green Discovery Band;
-   restrained gold/brass hardware.

Official Lawn With Care colors remain governed by official brand files.

## Materials --- APPROVED PRODUCTION REFERENCE

-   Shirt: natural cotton/canvas character; soft, breathable, matte
-   Shorts: durable cotton twill/canvas; matte and flexible
-   Discovery Band: durable woven canvas
-   Headband: soft matte cotton/fabric
-   Boots and belt: natural brown leather or production-equivalent
-   Hardware: warm brass/gold, preferably matte or satin
-   Badges: durable enamel/embroidered language as appropriate
-   Loupe: clear optical lens with simple durable frame

Avoid excessive shine, luxury finishes or futuristic materials.

## Relationship to Brooks --- CANON

Sprout is Brooks' discovery partner and learner. Brooks guides without
stealing her discoveries. Sprout should appear capable of participating
in investigations rather than simply following an adult.

Approximate production scale:

-   Sprout: **130--135 cm**
-   Brooks: **\~188 cm**

A dedicated relationship/scale sheet should become the authority for
shared staging.

## Known AI Failure Modes

### Character Drift

AI may alter age, height, face, curl pattern, hair length, eye color,
freckles or body proportions. Always use the Master Model Sheet as
primary visual reference.

### Discovery Band Drift

AI may alter band width, placement, ring design, badge count, badge
symbols or colors. Use the approved base system.

### Badge Invention

AI frequently creates plausible new badges. All new badges remain
proposals until explicitly approved.

### Prop Invention

AI may add explorer equipment. Use only canonical, approved or
story-authorized props.

### Wardrobe Drift

Do not allow the design to drift into scout uniforms, military styling,
fashion outfits or fantasy-explorer clothing.

### Brand Drift

AI may alter leaf count, frame geometry, typography, spacing, colors or
proportions in Lawn With Care artwork. Use official source assets for
final artwork.
