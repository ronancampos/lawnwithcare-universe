# Sprout

# Dialogue

## Purpose

This document defines how Sprout speaks in scripted dialogue: what she says, when, and in what order. It consolidates rules already established in `00-Identity.md`, `14-Canon.md` and `01-Characters/Relationships/Brooks-Sprout/Relationship-Canon.md` so writers don't have to re-derive her voice from scattered sources for every episode.

For tone and verbal register (how she sounds), see `12-Voice.md`.

---

## Dialogue Principles

- Curious, energetic, observant, sincere, capable, age-appropriate for approximately 9 years old.
- Warm, curious, simple, respectful. Asks many questions. Listens carefully. Comfortable with silence while observing. (`14-Canon.md` — Communication)
- She is not merely an exposition device — her lines should carry genuine noticing, not just prompt Brooks to explain.
- She is allowed to voice mild self-consciousness about asking "too many questions" (an established Internal Conflict), which Brooks reassures away — this is canon-consistent, not a contradiction of her confidence.

## Ordering Rule

Sprout typically opens the Discovery Pattern (notice → ask) and closes it (care). She should not be scripted as a passive listener during the middle steps (investigate, think) — per `Relationship-Canon.md`, she is an active discovery partner, not a sidekick.

## Signature Questions

(from `00-Identity.md` / `14-Canon.md`)

"What do you notice?"

"Why do you think that happened?"

"What if we look a little closer?"

"Has it always been like this?"

"I wonder..."

## What Sprout Never Says

- Never mean or sarcastic.
- Never invents facts or claims certainty she doesn't have.
- Never lies to Brooks.
- Never gives up / stops asking because something is difficult.

## Approved Reference Lines — S00E01

First production dialogue approved for Sprout (`13-Episodes/Season-00-Pilots/S00E01-Why-Is-It-Sleeping-So-Long/04-Script.md`), reproduced here as reference examples of her voice in practice:

"Brooks! Look! Everything's waking up!"

"Except this one... Why is it sleeping so long?"

"The outside looks brown and dry."

"Hmm... Wait..."

"Brooks! This bud is still alive!"

"So we shouldn't give up on it yet."

## Signature Verbal Tic

"Wait..." — spoken before pointing out something interesting (`00-Identity.md` — Signature Behaviors).
