# Sprout

# Visual DNA

> "Curiosity should be recognizable before the audience even sees her face."

---

# Purpose

This document defines Sprout's permanent visual identity.

It establishes the visual language that makes her instantly recognizable across comics, books, animation, educational materials and AI-generated artwork.

Every illustration of Sprout must remain consistent with these principles.

---

# Character Role

Sprout is the emotional heart of the Lawn With Care Universe.

She visually represents:

• Wonder

• Curiosity

• Discovery

• Hope

• Joy

• Growth

Whenever she appears, the audience should immediately feel invited to explore.

---

# Recognition Test

Sprout should remain recognizable when:

• viewed only as a silhouette

• viewed from behind

• reduced to a simple icon

• drawn in monochrome

• seen from a distance

If she cannot be identified instantly, the design should be revised.

---

# Visual Personality

Sprout should immediately communicate:

Curious

Gentle

Intelligent

Kind

Approachable

Observant

Hopeful

Adventurous

Never mischievous.

Never chaotic.

Her curiosity is intentional.

---

# Shape Language

Sprout is built almost entirely from rounded organic shapes.

Circles.

Soft curves.

Gentle asymmetry.

Nothing should feel rigid.

Nothing should feel sharp.

She visually represents life in constant growth.

---

# Silhouette

Sprout's silhouette is sacred.

Its defining elements are:

• Curly blonde hair

• Large round magnifying glass

• Junior Ranger sash

• Small rounded backpack

• Childlike proportions

• Curious posture

These elements define the character.

---

# Head

The head is intentionally slightly larger than realistic.

This improves:

Readability.

Expressiveness.

Emotional connection.

Her proportions should feel timeless.

---

# Face

Round face.

Soft cheeks.

Small rounded chin.

Friendly proportions.

She should always appear approachable.

---

# Eyes

Large.

Bright.

Blue-green.

Always curious.

They should constantly appear to be observing something.

Even while smiling.

---

# Eye Color

Official color:

Light blue-green.

Inspired by clear mountain lakes.

Never dark brown.

Never gray.

Never change between illustrations.

---

# Eyebrows

Soft.

Rounded.

Expressive.

Frequently raised in curiosity.

Never aggressive.

---

# Nose

Small.

Rounded.

Simple.

Natural.

---

# Mouth

Small smile.

Warm.

Often slightly open while discovering something.

Never exaggerated.

---

# Hair

Golden blonde.

Naturally curly.

Loose curls.

Soft volume.

Never perfectly styled.

The curls reinforce movement and curiosity.

---

# Hair Color

Official color:

Warm golden blonde.

Inspired by prairie grass illuminated during golden hour.

Never platinum.

Never orange.

Never ash blonde.

---

# Skin

Warm peach tone.

Official color:

#F0997B

Healthy outdoor complexion.

Natural.

---

# Body

Child proportions.

Slim but healthy.

Always energetic.

Never fragile.

Never exaggerated.

---

# Proportions

Approximately:

Head

1

Body

4 heads

Hands slightly oversized.

Feet slightly oversized.

These proportions improve readability.

---

# Posture

Sprout rarely stands perfectly still.

Her posture communicates curiosity.

Slight forward lean.

Head tilt.

Bent knees while observing.

Relaxed shoulders.

---

# Movement

Sprout moves lightly.

She frequently changes direction after noticing something.

She walks with purpose but often pauses unexpectedly to observe details.

She never runs aimlessly.

Curiosity guides every movement.

---

# Signature Pose

One knee slightly bent.

Magnifying glass held in front.

Head tilted.

Eyes focused on something tiny.

Gentle smile.

This pose should become iconic.

---

# Gesture Language

Sprout frequently:

Tilts her head.

Points excitedly at discoveries.

Holds her magnifying glass with both hands.

Squats to observe.

Touches leaves gently.

Looks upward while thinking.

Places one finger on her chin while wondering.

Avoid:

Hands on hips.

Crossed arms.

Aggressive pointing.

Exaggerated cartoon poses.

---

# Wardrobe Identity

Sprout always wears:

Forest green shirt.

Junior Yard Ranger sash.

Comfortable outdoor pants or shorts depending on season.

Rounded hiking shoes.

Small backpack.

Merit badge ring.

Magnifying glass.

Her clothing communicates exploration.

Never fashion.

# Junior Ranger Sash

The sash is Sprout's signature symbol.

It represents learning.

Not rank.

Not competition.

Badges celebrate discoveries.

Not superiority.

Future stories may add badges.

Existing badges are never removed.

---

# Merit Badge Ring

A recurring visual element.

Represents progress.

Every new badge reflects a meaningful learning experience.

The ring evolves throughout the series.

It is one of the few visual elements intentionally allowed to grow.

---

# Magnifying Glass

Sprout's magnifying glass is iconic.

Construction:

Rounded lens.

Simple wooden handle.

Warm natural finish.

Comfortable child proportions.

Never futuristic.

Never oversized.

Never redesigned.

---

# Backpack

Rounded.

Forest green.

Small.

Simple flap closure.

Subtle Discovery Frame emblem.

It should never appear overloaded.

---

# Materials

Cotton fabric.

Canvas.

Wood.

Leather accents.

Matte metal.

Natural materials reinforce the outdoor educational identity.

---

# Discovery Frame™

Whenever Sprout makes an important observation, the Discovery Frame may appear to highlight the detail.

Its appearance should feel like the audience is seeing the world through her attention.

Never use it randomly.

---

# Lighting

Preferred lighting:

Morning.

Golden hour.

Soft cloudy skies.

Filtered forest light.

Lighting should reinforce wonder.

Never dramatic.

Never dark.

---

# Camera Language

Preferred:

Eye level.

Ground perspective.

Close-ups.

Three-quarter views.

Over-the-shoulder while observing.

The camera should frequently adopt Sprout's point of view.

---

Hope.

Every expression should feel genuine.

---

# Recognition Checklist

Before approving any illustration ask:

✓ Is Sprout recognizable by silhouette?

✓ Is her magnifying glass present?

✓ Are her curls clearly visible?

✓ Is the sash correctly placed?

✓ Does she appear curious?

✓ Does she feel approachable?

✓ Does she inspire exploration?

If any answer is "No", revise the illustration.

---

# Visual Statement

Sprout does not represent childhood.

She represents the ability to notice what everyone else missed.

---

# Official Visual Principle

Wonder is visible before it is spoken.

Sprout should make the audience curious before she says a single word.
