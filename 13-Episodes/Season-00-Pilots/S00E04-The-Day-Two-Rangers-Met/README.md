# S00E04 --- The Day Two Rangers Met

Official production folder for the fourth Lawn With Care Universe
pilot episode. Category: series origin story (character/relationship,
not a science-topic episode).

## ⚠ Canon Status --- Read Before Producing

**This episode is `PROPOSED`, not `CANON`.** It establishes a specific
origin story for how Brooks and Sprout's partnership began ---
`Relationship-Canon.md` currently does not define this. Per
`AGENTS.md`'s Canon Change Policy, a new permanent relationship/origin
element requires explicit project-owner approval before it becomes
canon; producing this episode's script and artwork does **not**
automatically make the backstory official. Treat it as a produced,
approved-for-testing pilot, not locked history, until the owner
explicitly approves it (at which point `Relationship-Canon.md` should
be updated to reference it, and this notice removed).

Two smaller, explicitly flagged deviations from Sprout's usual visual
lock, both justified as one-time narrative devices and both reversed
by the end of the episode --- see `01-Concept-and-Narrative-Objective.md`:

1. Sprout does not yet carry her magnifying loupe --- she receives it
   from Brooks in the closing panel.
2. Sprout does not yet wear her Discovery Band --- consistent with
   this being the day her Discovery Band journey begins.

Both are permitted under `14-Canon.md`'s "Visual Identity" rule ("or a
narrative reason explaining their absence"), used here for exactly the
purpose that rule anticipates.

## Origin Note

Adapted from an external draft (`episode-test-C-origin-story-prompts.md`,
supplied by the project owner), originally written for an unnamed
"Yard Ranger" mascot and a "Seed stage" badge-progression system not
present in canon. Adapted here to use Ranger Brooks by name and
Sprout at her fixed canonical age and appearance (9 years old,
established design), rather than a younger/different-looking
"Seed stage" Sprout --- see "Canon Status" above for what was
preserved (missing loupe/Band as a one-time device) versus what was
changed (no separate age/design stage).
