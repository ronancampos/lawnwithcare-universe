# S00E04 --- The Day Two Rangers Met

## 01 --- Concept & Narrative Objective

> **Noticing before it's too late --- that's the whole job.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Canon Status

**PROPOSED. Not canon until the project owner explicitly approves it.**
See `README.md`, "Canon Status," for the full explanation. This
document defines the story as written for production testing; it does
not assert that this is officially how Brooks and Sprout met.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Premise

Before she was Brooks' discovery partner, Sprout was just a girl who
noticed a wilted flower nobody else seemed to see --- and wasn't sure
that noticing counted for anything. This episode is the origin of
their partnership, told through one quiet morning.

Unlike S00E01--S00E03, this episode carries no science Learning
Objective by design (matching the original draft's own note that
origin/character episodes can intentionally skip the science-lock
format). Its objective is emotional and identity-based, not factual.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Narrative Objective

**After the episode, the audience should understand:** noticing
something others walk past isn't a small thing --- it's the entire
skill a Yard Ranger is built on. You don't need equipment or training
to start; you need attention.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Central Emotional Question

**Does noticing something count, if you're not sure anyone else would
care?**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Sprout's State in This Episode

Sprout appears at her full canonical design (9 years old, established
face/hair/proportions --- see `01-Characters/Sprout/00-Identity.md` and
`14-Canon.md`) with two narratively-justified, temporary omissions:

- **No magnifying loupe yet** --- she receives it from Brooks in the
  closing panel, as the episode's central symbolic gift.
- **No Discovery Band yet** --- consistent with this being the very
  beginning of her badge journey; nothing to display yet.

Both omissions are permitted under `14-Canon.md`'s "Visual Identity"
rule and apply to this episode only --- every other produced episode
(S00E01--S00E03) shows her with both, and any future episode should
default to showing her with both unless there's a similarly explicit
narrative reason.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Brooks' State in This Episode

Brooks appears at his full canonical design, unchanged. His age is
intentionally never specified in canon ("should feel timeless" ---
`00-Identity.md`); this episode should not be staged in a way that
implies a specific number of years before/after S00E01--S00E03. Treat
it as "sometime before the other episodes," not "years ago."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Discovery Arc (emotional, not scientific)

```text
OBSERVE   — Sprout notices a wilted flower alone, unsure if it matters
QUESTION  — does anyone else see this? Am I the only one?
ARRIVAL   — Brooks arrives, kneels to her level, confirms he sees it too
VALIDATE  — "Good eye — most people walk right past a flower like this"
REALIZE   — Sprout recognizes her own noticing as something real
UNDERSTAND — "That's exactly what a Ranger does" — an identity, not a fact
BEGIN     — Brooks gives her the magnifying loupe — the journey starts
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Relationship Test

This episode is the foundation the Relationship Canon's principle
("Brooks guides. Sprout discovers.") is built on --- it should read as
the first instance of that dynamic, not a departure from it. Brooks
never leads with an answer; he confirms what Sprout already found.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# What This Episode Must NOT Do

- claim this is definitively "the" origin story until the owner
  approves it as canon (see "Canon Status" above);
- show Sprout as younger, smaller, or differently designed than her
  fixed canonical appearance;
- introduce a badge-progression/stage system not present in canon;
- give Sprout a name for the flower's problem or a scientific
  explanation --- this episode is about noticing, not diagnosis;
- make Brooks' first appearance feel like rescue or correction --- he
  arrives to affirm, not to fix.
