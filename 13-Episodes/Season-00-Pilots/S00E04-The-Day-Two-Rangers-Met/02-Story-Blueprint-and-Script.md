# S00E04 --- The Day Two Rangers Met

## 02 --- Story Blueprint & Script

> **You already know how to do the hardest part.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Format

Eight independent panels, 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 portrait, matching the
validated S00E01--S00E03 structure. Dialogue locked here.

# Discovery Ownership

Sprout notices and questions herself; Brooks' entire role is
validation, not instruction --- there is no "science" for him to
explain in this episode, which shifts his usual teaching beat (P07 in
the other episodes) into an identity-affirming beat instead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 01 --- A Quiet Front Yard

**Function:** ESTABLISH ENVIRONMENT

Wide shot, early morning light, a quiet front yard. Sprout stands
alone at the edge of the yard, looking uncertainly toward a wilted
flower bed nearby. Brooks is visible approaching from a distance down
the sidewalk, not yet close enough to speak.

**Dialogue:** caption only --- *"Before the Discovery Band. Before the
loupe."*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 02 --- Does Anybody Else See This?

**Function:** WONDER (uncertain, solitary --- distinct from
S00E01--S00E03's confident wonder, since Sprout doesn't yet have a
mentor validating her instincts)

Close-up: Sprout crouching alone beside the wilted flower bed, gently
touching a drooping petal, looking around as if checking whether
anyone else has noticed it too.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "Does anybody else see this? Or am I the only one who noticed
it was sad?"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 03 --- I See It Too

**Function:** ARRIVAL / VALIDATION

Brooks kneels down beside her at the flower bed, lowering himself to
her eye level, warm and genuinely interested --- his first real action
in the episode is to get low, not to speak first from standing height.

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "I see it too. Good eye --- most people walk right past a
flower like this without ever looking down."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 04 --- Somebody Should Notice

**Function:** EMOTIONAL CORE

Close-up: Sprout standing a little taller than before, a small,
surprised smile beginning to form --- the first flicker of pride at
having noticed something that mattered.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "I just thought... somebody should notice before it's too
late to help."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 05 --- That's Exactly What a Ranger Does

**Function:** UNDERSTAND (identity, not fact)

Brooks looking at her with a gentle, moved expression, as if he's just
recognized something important in her.

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "...That's exactly what a Ranger does. Noticing before it's
too late --- that's the whole job."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 06 --- You Already Know How

**Function:** CONFIRMATION (splitting the original longer line across
two panels for pacing, matching the validated no-panel-over-3-lines
density rule)

Same intimate framing continues, Brooks' expression softening further,
Sprout listening closely.

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "You already know how to do the hardest part."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 07 --- The Loupe

**Function:** BEGIN (symbolic gift --- this episode's equivalent of the
"understand" beat, since there's no science to explain)

Medium-wide shot: Brooks extending his own small magnifying glass
toward Sprout, warm proud smile. Sprout reaching up with both hands,
delighted, wide-eyed.

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "Every Ranger starts somewhere. Here --- start noticing with
this."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 08 --- Let's Find Out

**Function:** CLOSE / FORWARD HOOK

Sprout holding the loupe for the first time, looking through it at the
flower bed with Brooks beside her, both smiling --- the visual
beginning of the partnership shown in every other episode. Calmer,
settled energy.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "Let's find out what else I've been missing."

**Closing caption:** **EVERY RANGER STARTS BY NOTICING.**

# Dialogue Density Check

No panel exceeds 2 lines --- the lightest dialogue load of the four
produced pilots, appropriate to this episode's quieter, more intimate
register.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Deviation From the Original Draft (documented, not silent)

The original supplied draft used a distinct "Seed stage" design and a
7--9 age range for Sprout, and had Brooks confirm his line in one
longer combined panel. This version: locks Sprout to her single
canonical 9-year-old design throughout (no separate visual stage),
splits the longer confirmation line across P05/P06 to preserve the
validated dialogue-density limit, and adds an explicit closing caption
(matching the pattern established in S00E01--S00E03) where the
original relied on the final spoken line alone.
