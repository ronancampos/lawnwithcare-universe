# Season 00 --- Pilots

> "Test the system before scaling the universe."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Season 00 is the experimental production season of the Lawn With Care
Universe.

It exists to test, validate and improve the production system before the
official launch of Season 01.

Season 00 is not primarily designed to establish a large narrative arc.

Its primary purpose is to answer a practical question:

**Can the Lawn With Care Universe be produced repeatedly, consistently
and at a high enough quality to become a sustainable series?**

Every pilot should therefore test both:

1.  the story itself;
2.  the system used to create the story.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Season Status

Season 00 is:

**EXPERIMENTAL PRODUCTION**

Episodes may be published when approved, but production decisions
discovered during Season 00 remain subject to review before becoming
permanent franchise standards.

Season 00 may refine the production system.

Season 00 may not casually redefine established character canon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Rule

> **Test production. Protect canon.**

Season 00 may change:

-   episode length;
-   number of panels;
-   panel dimensions;
-   social-media format;
-   dialogue density;
-   pacing;
-   prompt structure;
-   image-generation workflow;
-   text-overlay workflow;
-   review procedures;
-   production templates;
-   file organization;
-   publishing workflow;
-   use frequency of visual devices such as the Discovery Frame.

Season 00 may NOT casually change:

-   Brooks' established identity;
-   Sprout's established identity;
-   approved Master Model Sheets;
-   core character values;
-   core relationship rules;
-   official Lawn With Care branding;
-   established franchise philosophy.

Any proposed canon change must go through an explicit canon decision
outside normal pilot iteration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Primary Production Questions

Season 00 should help answer:

## Story

-   What episode length works best?
-   How many panels should a standard episode contain?
-   How much dialogue can each panel support?
-   How quickly should the mystery appear?
-   How much explanation is appropriate?
-   Where should humor appear?
-   What makes the ending satisfying?
-   How strongly should each episode connect discovery to care?

## Characters

-   Can Brooks remain visually consistent across scenes?
-   Can Sprout remain visually consistent across scenes?
-   Can their relative scale remain stable?
-   Does their Relationship Canon work naturally in actual stories?
-   Does Sprout retain enough agency?
-   Does Brooks guide without dominating?
-   Do recurring gestures and props help recognition?

## Education

-   Is the scientific idea accurate?
-   Is it understandable without becoming simplistic?
-   Does the audience discover the idea instead of merely receiving an
    explanation?
-   Can observation lead naturally to evidence?
-   Is the learning objective clear after reading the episode?
-   Does the story encourage curiosity rather than memorization?

## Visual Production

-   Can characters remain consistent between generated panels?
-   Which reference assets are most effective?
-   Which prompts reduce visual drift?
-   Which camera compositions work best?
-   How much environmental detail is useful?
-   How should official brand assets be integrated?
-   Which recurring AI failure modes require workflow changes?

## Publishing

-   What format works best for Instagram?
-   What format works best for Facebook?
-   Should the same artwork support both?
-   Is a carousel the primary format?
-   Is a combined full-page version useful?
-   How much text remains readable on mobile?
-   What should appear in the post caption versus the artwork?

## Production Efficiency

-   How long does one episode take?
-   Which steps create the most rework?
-   Which steps can be templated?
-   Which steps require human approval?
-   Which assets can be reused?
-   Which tasks can be safely delegated to agents?
-   Where does AI save time?
-   Where does AI create additional correction work?

# Experimental Freedom

Season 00 is intentionally flexible.

A pilot may test:

-   6 panels;
-   8 panels;
-   different pacing;
-   different panel compositions;
-   different dialogue density;
-   different social-media crops;
-   different educational structures;
-   different prompt strategies.

A successful experiment may become a production standard.

An unsuccessful experiment should remain documented as a lesson.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Canon Boundary

Experimental freedom ends where established canon begins.

The following hierarchy remains authoritative:

1.  Approved Canon documents
2.  Approved Master Model Sheets
3.  Official Lawn With Care brand assets
4.  Approved Design Rules
5.  Approved Relationship Canon
6.  Approved production references
7.  Episode-specific production material
8.  Generated artwork

An episode cannot override higher-level canon simply because an image or
scene was successfully generated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Brooks During Season 00

Season 00 should test Brooks in real storytelling conditions while
preserving his established character.

Brooks should:

-   observe before explaining;
-   ask questions;
-   encourage evidence;
-   remain calm;
-   respect uncertainty;
-   treat Sprout as capable;
-   guide rather than lecture;
-   allow others to discover.

Brooks should not become an exposition machine simply because
educational information needs to be delivered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Sprout During Season 00

Season 00 should test Sprout as an active narrative force.

Sprout should:

-   notice;
-   question;
-   investigate;
-   make hypotheses;
-   use her magnifying loupe when appropriate;
-   make discoveries;
-   occasionally notice something Brooks missed;
-   participate meaningfully in conclusions.

Sprout must not become a passive child who exists only to ask Brooks
questions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Brooks + Sprout

Their shared production should follow the approved Relationship Canon.

Core principle:

**Brooks guides. Sprout discovers.**

The pilot season should specifically evaluate whether this principle
remains visible in:

-   dialogue;
-   staging;
-   composition;
-   eye-lines;
-   investigation;
-   discovery ownership;
-   conclusions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Discovery Structure

The preferred educational discovery pattern is:

**OBSERVE**

↓

**QUESTION**

↓

**INVESTIGATE**

↓

**THINK**

↓

**DISCOVER**

↓

**UNDERSTAND**

↓

**CARE**

Not every episode must display these stages mechanically.

However, the audience should generally experience discovery before
explanation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Nature's Role

Nature is not simply the background of Season 00.

Nature may function as:

-   mystery;
-   teacher;
-   evidence;
-   system;
-   living community;
-   source of surprise.

Whenever possible, the episode's central question should begin with
something observable in the natural world.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Scientific Integrity

Pilot status does not reduce scientific standards.

Each episode should distinguish between:

-   observation;
-   hypothesis;
-   evidence;
-   conclusion.

Scientific explanations must not be invented for narrative convenience.

If evidence is uncertain, characters may say:

**"I don't know yet."**

Uncertainty can create future discovery.

# Concept Gate

Before scripting, an episode concept should answer:

1.  What does Sprout notice?
2.  What is the mystery?
3.  Why would a child care?
4.  What can actually be observed?
5.  What is the central scientific idea?
6.  What discovery can Sprout participate in?
7.  What role does Brooks play?
8.  How does understanding lead to care?

If these cannot be answered clearly, the concept needs more development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Learning Objective Gate

Each episode should have one primary learning objective.

Secondary learning may occur, but the episode should not attempt to
teach too many unrelated concepts.

A good learning objective should be:

-   scientifically defensible;
-   observable;
-   age-appropriate;
-   connected to the mystery;
-   useful beyond the episode.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Story Blueprint Gate

Before writing full dialogue, the blueprint should establish:

-   opening image;
-   inciting observation;
-   central question;
-   initial hypothesis;
-   investigation;
-   evidence;
-   discovery;
-   explanation;
-   care/action;
-   closing emotional beat.

This prevents dialogue from determining story structure accidentally.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Script Gate

A script should be evaluated for:

-   character voice;
-   dialogue economy;
-   visual storytelling;
-   discovery ownership;
-   scientific accuracy;
-   emotional rhythm;
-   humor;
-   readability.

If the image can communicate something clearly, dialogue does not need
to repeat it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Panel Gate

Each panel should have a clear job.

Possible jobs include:

-   establish;
-   notice;
-   question;
-   compare;
-   investigate;
-   reveal;
-   explain;
-   react;
-   care;
-   close.

Avoid panels that exist only because a predetermined panel count must be
filled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Visual Production Gate

Before generating final artwork, verify:

-   Brooks reference;
-   Sprout reference;
-   relationship/scale reference;
-   required wardrobe;
-   required props;
-   environment;
-   camera;
-   emotional beat;
-   Discovery Frame usage;
-   brand requirements.

Generated images begin as:

**PROPOSED / UNVERIFIED**

They become final only after review.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# AI Failure Tracking

Season 00 should actively document recurring generation failures.

Examples:

-   Brooks face drift;
-   Sprout age drift;
-   incorrect height ratio;
-   wrong hair;
-   badge drift;
-   Discovery Band drift;
-   Brooks badge drift;
-   incorrect Lawn With Care logo;
-   invented props;
-   inconsistent wardrobe;
-   malformed text;
-   environmental continuity errors.

Repeated failures should improve prompts and workflow.

They should not modify canon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Brand Rule

Official Lawn With Care artwork must use official source assets.

Do not rely on generative AI to reproduce exact logos.

If branding is required in an illustration:

1.  generate the scene;
2.  use the official brand asset;
3.  composite it when necessary;
4.  verify final placement.

Brand presence should feel integrated rather than promotional unless the
asset is explicitly marketing-focused.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Discovery Frame

Season 00 may test the Discovery Frame's frequency and implementation.

The Discovery Frame should support focused observation.

It should not appear merely because it is a franchise element.

Questions to test:

-   Does it improve comprehension?
-   Does it help visual storytelling?
-   Is it intuitive?
-   Does it become repetitive?
-   When is it unnecessary?

Its core purpose remains protected even if implementation evolves.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Discovery Band

Season 00 may test how the Discovery Band functions narratively.

However:

new generated badges are not automatically canon.

A badge introduced during a pilot must be explicitly approved before
becoming part of the permanent badge system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Dialogue in Artwork

AI-generated lettering should not be trusted for production accuracy.

Preferred workflow:

1.  produce clean artwork;
2.  add speech bubbles and text through controlled composition;
3.  proofread;
4.  verify mobile readability.

Season 00 may test alternative workflows, but final dialogue must be
accurate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Social Media Testing

Season 00 should test production for social-first publishing.

Primary candidate:

**4:5 portrait carousel**

Typical target:

`1080 × 1350`

Possible secondary formats:

-   1:1 square;
-   combined page;
-   story/reel derivatives;
-   website presentation.

Exact publishing standards may be finalized after pilot results.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Production Metrics

Each pilot should record approximate:

-   planning time;
-   writing time;
-   image-generation time;
-   correction/re-generation time;
-   layout time;
-   review time;
-   total production time.

Optional metrics:

-   number of generations;
-   number of rejected images;
-   number of canon corrections;
-   number of brand corrections.

The goal is not merely speed.

The goal is predictable quality.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Post-Production Review

Every Season 00 episode should end with a short production
retrospective.

Recommended questions:

## What Worked?

What should be repeated?

## What Failed?

What caused rework?

## Character Consistency

Where did visual or behavioral drift occur?

## Educational Clarity

Was the learning objective achieved?

## Visual Clarity

Could each panel be understood quickly?

## AI Performance

Which prompts or references worked best?

## Workflow

Which production steps should change?

## Candidate Standard

What should potentially become a permanent production rule?

# Season 00 Success Criteria

Season 00 should not end simply because a predetermined number of
episodes has been produced.

It should end when the production system demonstrates sufficient
stability.

Indicators include:

-   Brooks remains consistently recognizable;
-   Sprout remains consistently recognizable;
-   shared scale is stable;
-   Relationship Canon works naturally;
-   scientific content is reliable;
-   stories remain entertaining;
-   dialogue fits the visual format;
-   image workflow is repeatable;
-   official branding remains accurate;
-   production time becomes predictable;
-   recurring AI failures have mitigation strategies;
-   episode structure is repeatable without becoming formulaic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Graduation to Season 01

Season 01 may begin when the project owner determines that the
production system is sufficiently validated.

Before graduation, Season 00 findings should be consolidated into
permanent production documentation.

Possible outputs include:

-   Episode Production Standard;
-   final panel template;
-   final social format;
-   revised prompt system;
-   updated AI workflow;
-   approved Discovery Frame rules;
-   approved Discovery Band implementation rules;
-   production checklist;
-   episode folder template.

Only validated lessons should move forward.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# What Season 00 Is Not

Season 00 is not:

-   an excuse for inconsistent characters;
-   a replacement for canon;
-   random experimentation;
-   disposable work;
-   a place to redesign the franchise every episode.

It is controlled experimentation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Season 00 Philosophy

We are not testing whether Brooks and Sprout should exist.

That foundation is already established.

We are testing:

**how to tell their stories consistently, clearly and beautifully.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Season 00 exists to discover the production system.

The characters should not change every time the system does.
