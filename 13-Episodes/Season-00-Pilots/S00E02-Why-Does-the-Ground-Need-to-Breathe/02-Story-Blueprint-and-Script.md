# S00E02 --- Why Does the Ground Need to Breathe?

## 02 --- Story Blueprint & Script

> **Give the ground room to breathe.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Format

Eight independent panels, 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 portrait, one scene per
file --- following the structure validated in S00E01
(`13-Episodes/Season-00-Pilots/S00E01-Why-Is-It-Sleeping-So-Long/03-Story-Blueprint.md`
v2.0). Dialogue is locked here and included in the first-draft
generation prompt for every panel (see `03-Codex-Draft-Generation-Brief.md`),
not deferred to a later lettering pass.

# Discovery Ownership

Sprout notices the contrast and performs the key test (feeling the
soil). Brooks redirects from assumption to evidence and supplies the
scientific explanation only after Sprout has found it.

# Revision Note --- v1.1 (2026-08-10)

v1.0 solved the mystery cleanly but read more like an explainer than a
story --- the discovery-cycle mechanics (notice → test → explain → fix)
were all present, but nothing in it required *this* particular child,
in *this* particular yard, to notice it. This revision adds one
change: **Sprout recognizes the worn path as her own** --- the one she
runs to the swing every day. The mystery stops being something she
observes from outside and becomes something she is quietly, gently
implicated in. Nothing else about the science or structure changes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 01 --- The Yard, Two Ways

**Function:** ESTABLISH ENVIRONMENT (no characters emphasized)

Wide shot of a well-loved backyard: a stepping/worn path cutting
across one section of lawn, thin and pale where feet cross it most,
lush green grass everywhere else. Late spring, warm clear daylight.
Brooks and Sprout may appear small/unposed entering the frame, or be
omitted.

**Dialogue:** caption only --- *"The paths we love most are the ones we
walk without noticing."*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 02 --- Everything Else Is Thriving

**Function:** WONDER

Sprout crouched in the lush section of lawn, delighted, running a
hand through thick healthy grass.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "Brooks, look how thick the grass is over here!"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 03 --- The Thin Strip

**Function:** MYSTERY / CONCERN

Sprout has moved to the worn path --- thin, patchy grass, some bare
soil visible. Puzzled concern, not alarm.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "But over here it's... kind of sad. Why won't it grow like the
rest?"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 04 --- What Do You Notice About the Ground?

**Function:** QUESTION / INVESTIGATION SETUP

Brooks kneels beside Sprout at the worn strip, conversational eye
level, redirecting from "why won't the grass grow" to "what's the
ground itself like."

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "Let's find out. What do you notice about the ground itself?"
SPROUT: "It feels harder. Like a sidewalk almost."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 05 --- Look Closer

**Function:** INVESTIGATE / FOCUS

Sprout pressing a finger into the worn soil, meeting resistance ---
close, focused investigation shot. Discovery Frame candidate: an
inset comparing the resistant worn soil against soft, crumbly healthy
soil.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "Whoa... my finger won't even go in."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 06 --- It's a Totally Different Ground

**Function:** DISCOVERY / SURPRISE

Sprout testing both patches side by side (or in quick succession),
delighted/surprised at the contrast --- then the delight settles into
something quieter as she recognizes exactly where she's standing.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "Brooks! Over here it's soft --- but this part is packed down
solid!"
SPROUT: "...Wait. This is my path. The one I take to the swing every
day."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 07 --- Understanding Compaction

**Function:** UNDERSTAND

Wider two-shot, Brooks explaining with a gentle gesture toward the
worn strip; Sprout offers her own relatable comparison. This mirrors
S00E01 P07's positive three-line pattern.

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "That's compaction. When soil gets packed too tight, there's
no room left for air or water to reach the roots."
SPROUT: "Like when everyone's squeezed onto the same bench, and I
can't breathe deep?"
BROOKS: "Exactly like that. Even a path we love needs room to breathe
too."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 08 --- Give It Room

**Function:** CARE / CLOSE

Brooks and Sprout together at the worn strip, calmer energy, perhaps
Brooks gesturing toward a simple aeration tool or the idea of poking
holes through the soil. No instant fix shown --- the strip is still
visibly worn.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "So we give it room. Maybe I'll take the long way to the swing
sometimes."
BROOKS: "That would help. And a few holes poked through the soil let
the air find its way back down to the roots."

**Closing caption:** **GIVE THE GROUND ROOM TO BREATHE.**

character rather than one crowded paragraph, matching P03's existing
two-line-single-speaker treatment. Matches the density profile
