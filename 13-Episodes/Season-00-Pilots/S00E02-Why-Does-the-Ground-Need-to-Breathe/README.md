# S00E02 --- Why Does the Ground Need to Breathe?

Official production folder for the second Lawn With Care Universe pilot
episode. Category: Soil & Roots --- soil compaction / aeration.

## Structural note (documented, per `Season-00-Pilots/README.md`'s
## explicit allowance to refine the episode template if justified)

S00E01 used seven separate pipeline documents. That granularity made
sense while the production *system itself* was unproven --- it isn't
needed to repeat verbatim for every subsequent episode now that the
system (8-panel structure, locked dialogue, `DRAFT_MATCH_MASTER` mode,
single-reference-per-character rule) is validated
(`08-Retrospective-Notes.md` in S00E01, and the `09-Codex-Draft-Generation.md`
pipeline audit). This episode consolidates the same content into three
documents instead of seven, to reduce the surface area for the kind of
cross-document contradiction that cost S00E01 several rework rounds.
