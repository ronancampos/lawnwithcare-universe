# S00E03 --- Too Much Love Can Drown a Root

## 02 --- Story Blueprint & Script

> **Love needs room to breathe.**

# Revision Note --- v1.1 (2026-08-10)

v1.0 already carried strong emotional material from the original
draft ("Can you love a plant too much?", "like a hug so tight I can't
move my arms") --- this episode had the most built-in poetic potential
of the three new pilots, since its premise is literally about love
expressed as excess. This revision leans further into that instead of
letting the ending resolve into a plain gardening tip: Brooks' P07
line now explicitly names love, not just soil, and the closing caption
changes from a practical instruction ("check the soil before you
water") to the episode's own emotional thesis ("love needs room to
breathe") --- which is also the more shareable, quotable line for
publication. No change to the science, structure, or panel count.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Format

Eight independent panels, 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 portrait, matching the
S00E01/S00E02-validated structure. Dialogue locked here, included in
first-draft generation prompts.

# Discovery Ownership

Sprout raises the emotionally real question ("can you love a plant too
much?"); Brooks validates it rather than dismissing it, then guides
the soil check that supplies the evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 01 --- A Garden Bed by the Porch

**Function:** ESTABLISH ENVIRONMENT

Wide shot: a home garden bed near a porch, overcast soft daylight, one
small plant visibly droopy and yellowed despite sitting in dark, damp
soil. Brooks and Sprout may appear small/unposed, or be omitted.

**Dialogue:** caption only --- *"Some things wilt not from too little care, but too much."*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 02 --- I Water It Every Day

**Function:** WONDER (tinged with quiet worry --- this episode's version
of the establishing beat carries more concern than S00E01/S00E02's,
since Sprout is already mid-care, not freshly discovering something)

Sprout kneeling beside the droopy plant, watering can in hand, gently
touching a yellowed leaf, confused and a little worried.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "I gave it water every single day. Why is it still sad? Can
you love a plant too much?"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 03 --- You Can

**Function:** VALIDATION / MYSTERY DEEPENS

Brooks arrives, kneeling beside her, taking her question seriously
rather than correcting her instinct.

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "You can. Roots don't just drink water --- they breathe through
the tiny spaces around them."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 04 --- What the Soil Shows

**Function:** INVESTIGATION SETUP

Brooks gently presses into the damp soil, showing Sprout how saturated
it is, guiding her attention from the plant to the ground it's in.

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "Too much water fills up all that space, and there's no air
left for the roots at all."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 05 --- Look Closer

**Function:** INVESTIGATE / FOCUS

Close, focused shot: Sprout's own fingers pressing into the soil,
feeling the saturation herself --- this is her hands-on confirmation of
what Brooks just described. Discovery Frame candidate: cross-section
inset showing water filling root spaces vs. a healthy aerated
comparison.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "It's really squishy... like a sponge."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 06 --- It's Not Thirsty

**Function:** DISCOVERY / SURPRISE

Sprout's moment of realization --- the plant isn't dry at all, the
opposite of what she assumed.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "So it's not thirsty at all... it's drowning?"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 07 --- Real Care Checks First

**Function:** UNDERSTAND

Wider two-shot, Brooks confirming and connecting to the emotional
core --- reusing the validated positive three-line pattern, with
Sprout's own relatable analogy.

**Dialogue:**
BROOKS: "Exactly. Even love needs to leave room to breathe."
SPROUT: "Like when someone hugs me so tight I can't move my arms?"
BROOKS: "Exactly like that."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PANEL 08 --- Check Before You Water

**Function:** CARE / CLOSE

Calmer closing composition. Sprout's watering can set aside; she rests
her fingers near the base of the plant instead of reaching for water.
Brooks watching with a warm, proud smile. The plant is still visibly
droopy --- no instant recovery.

**Dialogue:**
SPROUT: "So we check first. Every time."
BROOKS: "Every time. Not because we love it less --- because we're
paying attention."

**Closing caption:** **LOVE NEEDS ROOM TO BREATHE.**
