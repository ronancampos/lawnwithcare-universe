# Lawn With Care Universe — Project Context

## Purpose

This repository is the official knowledge base and single source of truth for the Lawn With Care Universe.

It contains the franchise canon, character definitions, world building, storytelling rules, visual direction, production systems, AI instructions and episode development materials.

Any human or AI collaborator working on the project must treat the repository as authoritative.

Conversation history, memory, assumptions or external model knowledge must never override documented canon.

---

# Core Franchise Idea

The Lawn With Care Universe is an original educational family franchise built around curiosity, observation, scientific thinking and care for nature.

Its central creative principle is:

> The extraordinary already exists. We simply help people notice it.

Nature is the true protagonist.

Stories reveal real-world wonder rather than inventing fantasy to make nature interesting.

# Core Characters

## Ranger Brooks

Brooks is the adult mentor and Yard Ranger.

He represents:

- method
- patience
- observation
- scientific thinking
- care
- leadership through example

Brooks guides discovery without stealing it.

He does not lecture.

He helps others investigate.

Authoritative documents are located under:

`01-Characters/Brooks/`

---

## Sprout

Sprout is the child protagonist and emotional center of discovery.

She represents:

- wonder
- curiosity
- observation
- fresh perspective
- growth
- hope

Sprout notices what adults often overlook.

Her curiosity begins many stories.

Authoritative documents are located under:

`01-Characters/Sprout/`

---

# Brooks + Sprout

The franchise depends on their complementary perspectives.

Sprout notices.

Brooks investigates.

Together they understand.

Understanding creates care.

Neither character should replace the role of the other.

# Lawn With Care

Lawn With Care is the real-world sponsor and visual inspiration behind the franchise.

Its identity influences:

- color palette
- visual symbols
- philosophy of care
- professionalism
- Discovery Frame
- broader franchise design language

The fictional stories must never become advertisements.

The brand supports the universe quietly through values and design.

# Yard Rangers

The Yard Rangers are an educational and stewardship-oriented organization inside the fictional universe.

They are:

- naturalists
- mentors
- observers
- educators
- caretakers of curiosity

They are not:

- police
- military
- superheroes
- authority figures based on power

# Discovery System

The official discovery cycle is:

Observe  
↓  
Wonder  
↓  
Question  
↓  
Investigate  
↓  
Compare  
↓  
Understand  
↓  
Share  
↓  
Care

Observation always comes before explanation.

Scientific thinking is part of the story, not an interruption to it.

---

# Storytelling Principles

A Lawn With Care story should:

- begin with something ordinary
- invite observation
- create a question
- include investigation
- reward attention
- preserve scientific integrity
- contain one meaningful discovery
- lead toward care
- end with renewed curiosity

Stories should not depend on villains, spectacle or artificial danger.

Hope is mandatory.

# Working Rule

Do not invent missing canon silently.

If the repository does not define something:

1. identify the gap;
2. propose an option;
3. label it as a proposal;
4. do not treat it as canon until approved and documented.
