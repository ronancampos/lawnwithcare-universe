# 🌱 Lawn With Care Universe

> **Official Franchise Bible & Knowledge Base**

Welcome to the official knowledge repository of the **Lawn With Care Universe**.

This repository contains the official canon, visual language, storytelling rules, character bibles, production guides, AI prompts, design system, educational philosophy, and every creative asset required to produce consistent content across books, comics, animation, games, social media and educational material.

This repository is the **single source of truth** for the franchise.

---

# Mission

Create a timeless educational entertainment franchise that inspires children and families to observe nature more carefully, ask better questions, and discover the extraordinary hidden inside ordinary backyards.

Learning happens through curiosity.

Curiosity creates care.

Care changes the world.

---

# Franchise Philosophy

The Lawn With Care Universe believes that:

Nature is full of stories.

Every backyard is an ecosystem.

Every child can become a discoverer.

Adults do not teach all the answers.

Adults learn together with children.

Wonder comes before knowledge.

Observation comes before explanation.

Respect comes before intervention.

# Discovery Frame™

The Discovery Frame is the official narrative symbol of the Lawn With Care Universe.

It is directly inspired by the official Lawn With Care logo.

Its proportions, geometry and visual language never change.

The Discovery Frame represents:

Observation

Discovery

Learning

Care

Whenever something deserves attention, it belongs inside the Discovery Frame.
