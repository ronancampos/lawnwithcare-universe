# 🌱 Lawn With Care Universe

# Design System

---

> **Every visual decision should make the universe feel more recognizable, never merely more beautiful.**

---

# Purpose

The Design System defines the permanent visual language of the Lawn With Care Universe.

Its purpose is to ensure that every illustration, comic, animation, educational activity, marketing piece and digital product feels like it belongs to the same universe.

This document complements the Visual DNA.

Visual DNA explains *why*.

The Design System explains *how*.

---

# Design Principles

Every design decision should satisfy five principles:

1. Clarity
2. Warmth
3. Curiosity
4. Readability
5. Recognition

If a design is visually impressive but less readable, simplify it.

Recognition always wins.

---

# The Recognition Rule

Every recurring element should be identifiable by silhouette alone.

Hide all colors.

Hide all details.

If the silhouette remains recognizable, the design is successful.

- reliability

Field Kit

Ranger Station

Signs

---

## Triangle

Represents:

- attention
- movement
- discovery

Used only as an accent.

---

## Organic Shapes

Everything living should contain slight asymmetry.

Perfect geometry belongs to manufactured objects.

Imperfection communicates life.

---

# Character Construction

Characters are built using large readable shapes.

Avoid excessive anatomical complexity.

Head-to-body ratio:

Children

1 : 4

Adults

1 : 5.5

Hands slightly oversized.

Eyes larger than realistic.

Feet slightly enlarged for stability.

---

# Silhouette Rules

Every major character must have:

One unique head shape.

One unique body shape.

One unique accessory.

One unique standing posture.

Recognition should happen instantly.

# Color Hierarchy

Characters receive the strongest saturation.

Important objects receive medium saturation.

Backgrounds receive lower saturation.

Nothing should compete with the story.

---

# Line Weight

Primary outlines:

Medium.

Internal details:

Thin.

Decorative lines:

Minimal.

Avoid noisy illustrations.

---

# Rendering Style

Soft shadows.

Minimal gradients.

Flat local colors.

Warm ambient light.

Visible shape separation.

Readable at thumbnail size.

# Iconography

Icons use:

Rounded corners.

Simple geometry.

Flat colors.

Single visual metaphor.

Icons should be understandable without text.

---

# Discovery Frame™

The Discovery Frame is the primary recurring graphic element of the franchise.

Rules:

Never distort.

Never rotate arbitrarily.

Never crop its characteristic shape.

Never replace it with another frame.

Always use it intentionally.

It indicates focused observation.

---

# Badges

Every badge follows the same construction:

Circular.

Gold outer ring.

Cream base.

Green primary symbol.

One central concept.

No text required.

Badges represent learning, not ranking.

---

# Notebook

The field notebook is always:

Cream pages.

Soft gray lines.

Gold lower stripe.

Forest green cover.

Rounded corners.

This design never changes.

---

# Field Kit

The diagnostic field kit is always:

Forest green body.

Gold latch.

Rounded edges.

Compact proportions.

Clean interior organization.

Scientific—not mechanical.

---

# Magnifying Glass

Sprout's magnifying glass is iconic.

Simple wooden handle.

Rounded lens.

No decorative details.

Never futuristic.

Never oversized.

---

# Backpack

Sprout's backpack is always:

Rounded.

Forest green.

Small enough for a child.

Simple flap closure.

Subtle Discovery Frame emblem.

---

# Ranger Hat

Brooks' campaign hat is sacred.

It defines his silhouette.

Never remove it without story justification.

Never redesign it.

---

# Boots

Black.

Rounded.

Functional.

Comfortable.

Not military.

Not tactical.

---

# Trees

Trees should always feel inviting.

Rounded canopies.

Organic trunks.

Visible asymmetry.

Different species remain recognizable.

---

# Grass

Grass is never a flat carpet.

Small variation.

Movement.

Different heights.

Healthy ecosystems feel alive.

---

# Rocks

Rounded.

Friendly.

Weathered.

Never sharp unless geologically important.

---

# Buildings

Simple.

Wooden.

Natural materials.

Large windows.

Open spaces.

Welcoming architecture.

---

# Vehicles

Vehicles support exploration.

They never become the focus.

No aggressive styling.

No racing aesthetics.

Practical.

Friendly.

Functional.

---

# Typography

Whenever text appears inside the universe:

Friendly.

Readable.

Educational.

Never decorative.

Never futuristic.

Never gothic.

---

# Composition Rules

Every illustration must have:

Primary focal point.

Secondary focal point.

Visual breathing space.

Clear reading direction.

No unnecessary clutter.

---

# Motion Language

Movement follows arcs.

Nothing moves rigidly.

Plants sway.

Children bounce slightly.

Brooks walks confidently.

Nature always feels alive.

---

# Repetition

Visual repetition creates familiarity.

Repeat:

Discovery Frame.

Gold accents.

Rounded corners.

Natural textures.

Leaf motifs.

Notebook.

Field Kit.

Badges.

These recurring elements become visual anchors.

---

# Things That Must Never Change

The Discovery Frame.

Brooks' hat.

Sprout's magnifying glass.

Official palette.

Rounded organic style.

Warm emotional tone.

Educational purpose.

Nature-first compositions.

---

# Design Review Checklist

Before approving any illustration ask:

✓ Does it feel warm?

✓ Does it encourage curiosity?

✓ Is the Discovery Frame correctly used?

✓ Are silhouettes recognizable?

✓ Does nature feel alive?

✓ Are the colors consistent?

✓ Would this still be recognizable without the logo?

If any answer is "No", revise the artwork.

---

# Final Principle

People should recognize the Lawn With Care Universe before they notice its logo.

Recognition comes from consistency.

Consistency builds trust.

Trust creates timeless franchises.
