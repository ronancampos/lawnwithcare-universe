# Brainstorm Kickoff Prompt

Ready-to-paste prompts for generating new Lawn With Care Universe
episode ideas with any AI tool. Paste `11-AI-Brainstorm-Story-Bible.md`
into the chat first (as a file attachment or pasted text), then use
one of the prompts below.

---

## Prompt A --- Generate New Episode Ideas

```text
Using the attached Lawn With Care Universe Story Bible as your only
source of canon, propose 5 new episode ideas.

For each idea, give me:
1. Title (a question or evocative phrase, matching the style of the
   produced episodes)
2. The small, ordinary observation that starts it (never a big event)
3. The central mystery question
4. The real nature/science fact involved (must be genuinely accurate
   -- flag anything you're not fully certain about rather than
   guessing)
5. The personal-stakes twist -- how does Sprout end up implicated in
   the mystery, not just observing it from outside?
6. The felt/human analogy that makes the invisible mechanism visible
   (something Sprout has felt in her own body or heart)
7. A closing tagline with double meaning (literal AND metaphorical)
8. Which existing Discovery Badge fits, or note if none does

Before including an idea, run it through the Brooks Test, the Sprout
Test, and the Walk Outside Test from the Story Bible. Reject and
replace any idea that fails.

Avoid overlapping premises or emotional territory with the four
already-produced episodes listed in the Story Bible.
```

---

## Prompt B --- Deepen One Existing Idea

```text
Using the attached Lawn With Care Universe Story Bible, help me
develop this episode idea further: [describe your rough idea in 1-3
sentences].

Specifically:
1. Sharpen it into a single mystery question.
2. Suggest the personal-stakes twist that implicates Sprout directly
   (per the Story Bible's "Little Prince Lens" section) -- don't
   settle for her simply observing something from outside.
3. Suggest the felt/human analogy a 9-year-old would actually reach
   for.
4. Draft 3 alternative closing taglines, each with a literal AND a
   metaphorical meaning. Tell me which one you'd pick and why.
5. Sanity-check the science: is this accurate? What should Brooks NOT
   claim, to avoid overstating the science?
```

---

## Prompt C --- Draft the 8-Beat Script

```text
Using the attached Lawn With Care Universe Story Bible, write a full
8-beat script for this episode idea: [paste the idea, including its
tagline].

Follow the Episode Formula section exactly: Establish, Wonder,
Mystery/Concern, Investigate Setup, Investigate/Focus, Discovery,
Understand, Care/Close. Sprout owns the discovery; Brooks never
supplies the answer before she's found the evidence. Keep dialogue
light -- most beats get one short line, none should exceed three.
Close on the tagline.

After the script, list which existing Discovery Badge fits, and note
any scientific claims that should be double-checked before this goes
into production.
```

---

## Reminder

Anything generated this way is a **draft proposal**. It is not
official canon until the project owner reviews and approves it --- say
so explicitly if you're sharing generated ideas with anyone else.
